## The Big Picture: What Is a "Theory of Everything"?

Before comparing, let's be clear about what we're comparing *to*. A "Theory of Everything" (TOE) is a hypothetical theory that:
1. Unifies all four fundamental forces (gravity, electromagnetism, strong nuclear, weak nuclear).
2. Reconciles quantum mechanics with general relativity.
3. Explains all fundamental particles and their interactions.
4. Provides a complete, mathematically consistent description of physical reality.

No current theory meets all these criteria. The leading candidates are:
- **String Theory** (and its variants: M-theory, F-theory, etc.)
- **Loop Quantum Gravity** (LQG) and its variants
- **Causal Set Theory**
- **Causal Dynamical Triangulations (CDT)**
- **Asymptotic Safety** in quantum gravity
- **Emergent Gravity** approaches (Verlinde, Jacobson)
- **Pilot-wave / Bohmian extensions to quantum gravity**
- **Superdeterminism / RQM-style approaches to quantum gravity**

Each of these has *some* claim to be a TOE candidate. None is complete.

The "Bohm + RQM synthesis" I'm discussing here is not yet a published, worked-out TOE. It's a *speculative program* that combines Bohmian mechanics (which has well-developed non-relativistic foundations) with Palmer's invariant set approach (which is more conceptual and less developed). To be fair, I need to evaluate it *as a research program*, not as a finished theory.

## Part 1: Strengths of the Bohm + RQM Synthesis

### 1. It Directly Addresses the Quantum Measurement Problem

**What it offers:** Both Bohm and RQM take the measurement problem seriously and offer *deterministic resolutions*. A TOE needs to be consistent with the quantum world, and quantum mechanics has a measurement problem that most TOE candidates *ignore*. String theory, for instance, takes QM as given and tries to add gravity. It doesn't tell you what happens during measurement. The Bohm + RQM synthesis has a clear story: measurement reveals pre-existing properties, guided by a pilot wave, constrained by an invariant set.

**Why this matters:** A TOE that doesn't address the measurement problem is incomplete in a deep way. The Bohm + RQM synthesis is one of the few programs that takes the foundational problem seriously.

**Comparison:** String theory and LQG largely *bracket* the measurement problem. They focus on the high-energy / Planck-scale physics and leave the interpretation of QM to philosophers. The Bohm + RQM synthesis puts the measurement problem at the center.

### 2. It Provides a Clear Ontological Picture

**What it offers:** Bohm gives a clear ontology: particles + wave function. RQM adds geometric structure: the invariant set. Together, they offer a *layered* ontology: at the level of dynamics, particles and pilot wave; at the level of structure, the invariant set. This is more *ontologically explicit* than most TOE candidates.

**Why this matters:** Many TOE candidates (especially string theory) have unclear ontologies. What is a string? Is it real, or just a calculational tool? What is a spin network in LQG? The Bohm + RQM synthesis is more honest about *what exists*.

**Comparison:** String theory is often criticized for lacking a clear ontology. LQG has a clearer ontology (spin networks are real), but the relationship to familiar objects (particles, fields) is not crisp. The Bohm + RQM synthesis is clearer about what the world is *made of*.

### 3. It Naturally Handles Nonlocality

**What it offers:** Both Bohm and RQM accept nonlocality as a feature, not a bug. A TOE needs to be consistent with the nonlocal features of QM (Bell inequality violations, entanglement), and these programs have a story: the pilot wave is explicitly nonlocal, and the invariant set has a nonlocal structure.

**Why this matters:** Many physicists *hope* a TOE will restore locality. But Bell's theorem says you can't have local realism. A TOE that tries to restore locality is in tension with the empirical facts. The Bohm + RQM synthesis is honest about nonlocality and builds it into the foundations.

**Comparison:** String theory and LQG *hope* to restore locality at the Planck scale, but the empirical evidence (Bell violations) suggests this hope is unfounded. The Bohm + RQM synthesis doesn't fight the empirical facts.

### 4. It Connects to Cosmology and the Early Universe

**What it offers:** The invariant set idea is naturally cosmological. The "initial conditions" of the universe are part of the invariant set, and the geometric structure of the set is what gives the universe its specific features (the Born rule, the arrow of time, etc.). The Bohm part provides the dynamics by which the universe evolves from these initial conditions.

**Why this matters:** A TOE needs to explain the initial conditions of the universe. Standard approaches either say "the initial conditions were set by inflation" (which is unsatisfying) or "all initial conditions are realized in the multiverse" (which is unfalsifiable). The Bohm + RQM synthesis offers a third option: the initial conditions are *constrained* by the invariant set structure.

**Comparison:** String theory's multiverse is widely criticized as unfalsifiable. LQG's approach to initial conditions is underdeveloped. The Bohm + RQM synthesis offers a *specific* geometric story, which is at least in principle testable.

### 5. It Has a Built-in Solution to the Problem of "Why These Laws?"

**What it offers:** The invariant set structure is not itself a *law* — it's a *geometric fact* about which states are possible. So the question "why these laws?" is reframed as "why this invariant set?" This is still a deep question, but it's a *different* deep question — and one that might be more tractable. The pilot wave dynamics is then the *law* that governs evolution *within* the allowed states.

**Why this matters:** The "fine-tuning" problem is one of the deepest puzzles in physics. Why do the constants of nature have the values they do? Why do the laws take the form they do? Most TOE candidates don't address this — they just *assume* certain laws and constants. The Bohm + RQM synthesis offers a framework where this question becomes geometric, not just nomic.

**Comparison:** String theory's multiverse is one attempt to address fine-tuning (all constants are realized somewhere). LQG doesn't really address it. Asymptotic Safety addresses some of it (the running of couplings to a fixed point). The Bohm + RQM synthesis offers a *geometric* approach.

### 6. It's Philosophically Coherent and Conservative

**What it offers:** The synthesis preserves realism (the world is *made of stuff* with definite properties). It preserves determinism (the future is fixed by the past). It preserves mathematical structure. It is, in a sense, *conservative* — it tries to retain as much of the classical worldview as possible while accounting for the quantum facts.

**Why this matters:** A TOE that abandons realism or determinism is harder to interpret, harder to extend, and harder to use as a foundation for further physics. The Bohm + RQM synthesis is more *usable* as a research framework than, say, many-worlds, which is realist in a strange sense (all branches are real) but hard to work with.

**Comparison:** Many-worlds is also deterministic and realist, but its ontology is extravagant (an infinity of branching universes). The Bohm + RQM synthesis is more parsimonious: one universe, with a hidden structure.

## Part 2: Weaknesses, Flaws, and Gaps

### 1. The Relativistic Field-Theoretic Extension Is Not Developed

**The gap:** Bohmian mechanics works beautifully for non-relativistic quantum mechanics. It has been extended to bosonic field theory (Durr, Goldstein, Tumulka, Zanghì) and to fermions (with some technical issues). But a full relativistic quantum field theory with a pilot wave is not yet in hand. For a TOE, you need quantum *field* theory, not just particle mechanics. This is a major gap.

**Why it matters:** A TOE must include the standard model of particle physics, which is a relativistic quantum field theory. Without a working pilot-wave QFT, the synthesis cannot claim to be a TOE.

**Comparison:** String theory and LQG both have (limited) handles on quantum field theory and quantum gravity. The Bohm + RQM synthesis is behind.

### 2. Quantum Gravity Is Not Addressed

**The gap:** Neither Bohm nor RQM has a clear story about quantum gravity. The invariant set idea is suggestive (it could be relevant at the Planck scale), but no detailed model exists. The pilot-wave dynamics for gravity (curved spacetime, etc.) is not worked out. There is no analogue of the Hawking radiation calculation in pilot-wave gravity.

**Why it matters:** A TOE *must* address quantum gravity. This is the central challenge of contemporary fundamental physics. Without a story about quantum gravity, the synthesis cannot be a TOE.

**Comparison:** String theory and LQG are *built* around quantum gravity. They may not have succeeded, but at least they're trying. Causal set theory is similarly focused. The Bohm + RQM synthesis has nothing comparable.

### 3. The Invariant Set Structure for Realistic Quantum Systems Is Unknown

**The gap:** Palmer's invariant set idea is well-developed for *simple* systems (the "two-cell" attractor for the Lorenz system, simple low-dimensional quantum systems). But for *realistic* quantum systems (a hydrogen atom, let alone a QFT), the invariant set structure is not known. It might be too complicated to compute, or it might not exist in the form Palmer envisions.

**Why it matters:** Without a concrete invariant set for realistic systems, RQM remains a *program*, not a *theory*. It's not clear that the framework can be made rigorous.

**Comparison:** String theory has a precise mathematical structure (conformal field theory, Calabi-Yau manifolds, etc.). LQG has a precise mathematical structure (spin networks, spin foams). The Bohm + RQM synthesis is mathematically vague at the most critical point.

### 4. The Measurement Independence Problem Is Not Solved (Just Relabeled)

**The gap:** RQM denies strict measurement independence, but it doesn't really *solve* the problem — it just *relabels* it as "geometric constraint." The question "why this invariant set?" is just as hard as "why these correlations?" You haven't eliminated the fine-tuning; you've *moved* it to a different level.

**Why it matters:** Critics of superdeterminism (including Bell) argued that any theory denying measurement independence is unfalsifiable and conspiratorial. RQM tries to escape this by appealing to geometry, but the *basic move* — denying the experimenter's freedom — is still there. Whether the geometric framing makes it more palatable is a matter of taste.

**Comparison:** String theory doesn't address measurement independence at all. LQG doesn't either. The Bohm + RQM synthesis is in the awkward position of being more *committed* to a controversial move than most TOE candidates.

### 5. Empirical Testability Is Weak

**The gap:** Both Bohm and RQM reproduce all standard QM predictions. They make no new testable predictions (in their current forms). A TOE that makes no new predictions is not falsifiable, and a TOE that is not falsifiable is not science (by Popper's criterion).

**Why it matters:** A TOE should be *in principle* testable. If the synthesis is empirically equivalent to standard QM + GR for all foreseeable experiments, it is not a *scientific* theory in the strong sense — it's a *metaphysical* framework.

**Comparison:** String theory has struggled with testability (no experimental confirmation in 40+ years). LQG has the same problem. But at least *in principle*, these theories make predictions (e.g., supersymmetric particles, signatures in CMB). The Bohm + RQM synthesis, in its current form, makes *zero* new predictions.

### 6. The Synthesis Itself Is Speculative

**The gap:** I have been discussing a "Bohm + RQM synthesis" as if it were a developed theory. It is not. It is a *speculative construction* on my part (and partly on Palmer's). No published paper works out the full synthesis. The mathematical consistency is not established. The two frameworks have different formal structures and have not been shown to fit together cleanly.

**Why it matters:** You can't evaluate a theory that doesn't exist. The Bohm + RQM synthesis is more of a *hope* than a *theory* at this point.

**Comparison:** String theory is a *developed* (if incomplete) theory. LQG is a *developed* (if incomplete) theory. The synthesis is not even at the level of a *developed incomplete* theory.

### 7. The Pilot Wave Is a Configuration-Space Object

**The gap:** In Bohmian mechanics, the wave function lives in 3N-dimensional configuration space (where N is the number of particles). This is ontologically extravagant: a 10²⁸-dimensional space for a speck of dust. For QFT, the situation is worse: the wave function lives in an infinite-dimensional space. This is mathematically tractable (Durr et al. have shown how to handle it) but philosophically troubling. Is configuration space real? Or just a mathematical tool?

**Why it matters:** A TOE should have a clear ontology. If the wave function lives in an ontologically extravagant space, that's a problem.

**Comparison:** String theory's strings live in 10 or 11 dimensions — also ontologically extravagant, but at least the extra dimensions might be "compactified." LQG's spin networks are combinatorial and don't have this problem. The Bohm + RQM synthesis inherits the configuration-space problem from Bohm.

### 8. The Free-Will Issue Remains Contested

**The gap:** Whether RQM's geometric denial of free choice is more palatable than superdeterminism's bare denial is a matter of debate. Some philosophers think it is; others think it is just "conspiracy with extra steps." The synthesis doesn't resolve this — it inherits it from RQM.

**Why it matters:** The "free will" issue is not just philosophical — it touches on the scientific method. If we cannot freely choose experiments, science as we know it is undermined. A TOE that undermines science is in a strange position.

**Comparison:** Most TOE candidates don't address this. String theory, LQG, etc. all *assume* the experimenter is free to choose measurements. The Bohm + RQM synthesis is unusual in taking the issue seriously — but its solution is controversial.

## 0. Executive Summary

EBP v2.1 is the minimal operational form of the Elephant Bridge Protocol.

Its core doctrine is:

```text
Ideas enter free.
Promotion costs debt.
Debt does not kill.
Debt is forever payable.
New evidence creates new debt.
No final-truth claim may be promoted.
Accounting must never become the work.
```

The protocol exists to protect two things simultaneously:

1. **The theorist's imagination.** Any idea may enter as a one-line claim.
2. **The search's epistemic integrity.** No idea may be promoted until its unpaid obligations are visible and sufficiently retired.

EBP v2.1 is not a theory of everything, not a theory generator, not a truth engine, not a replacement for peer review, not a Lean supremacy doctrine, and not a new gatekeeping institution.

It is a **notebook with a conscience**.

A notebook preserves imagination.  
A conscience prevents overclaiming.

The shortest summary is:

```text
Door: wide open.
Throne: guarded by debt.
```

Ideas enter through the door.  
Only debt-paid ideas approach the throne.

## 1. Why v2.1 Exists

EBP v1 preserved the original Blind Men and the Elephant spirit: each theory may touch a real part of the whole, but no theory should be accepted wholesale or rejected wholesale without extracting the features that survive.

EBP v2 corrected a deeper problem: even the protocol's own favorite assumptions can become dogma. TBA, E8, optimality, Bohmian decomposition, holography, relational amplitude spaces, and Lean formalization may be valuable tools or candidate lenses, but none should be sacred.

EBP v2.1 corrects the operational problem: a protocol designed to fight confusion can itself become bureaucracy if it demands too much structure too early.

Therefore v2.1 separates **entry** from **promotion**.

```text
Entry is cheap.
Promotion is expensive.
```

An idea may enter with no map, no invariant, no Lean object, no null model, no obstruction, and no proof.

But it may not be promoted while those debts remain unpaid.

This is the central anti-bureaucracy move.

```lean
inductive DebtItem
| needMap
```

```lean
def ideaAlive (_ : Idea) : Bool :=
```

```text
Every idea is alive.
```

## 6. Debt Items and How to Retire Them

Debt retirement is not bureaucracy. It is one useful move at a time.

```text
One useful move retires one debt.
```

### 6.1 `needMap`

An idea owes a map when it claims a relation between structures, theories, regimes, or descriptions.

Examples:

```text
E8 structure -> gauge symmetry
boundary area -> information capacity
spin-network punctures -> entropy contribution
CFT state count -> boundary distinguishability
```

Minimal retirement condition:

```text
State domain, codomain, and translation rule.
```

Lean shape:

```lean
structure CandidateMap where
  Domain : Type
  Codomain : Type
  map : Domain -> Codomain
```

A candidate map does not prove the bridge. It only makes the claim inspectable.

### 6.2 `needInvariant`

An idea owes an invariant when it claims that something survives translation, emergence, approximation, duality, reconstruction, or comparison.

Examples:

```text
capacity
entropy scaling
spectrum
symmetry
causal order
correlation structure
anomaly cancellation
code distance
```

Minimal retirement condition:

```text
State the invariant sharply enough that it can be preserved, violated, or compared.
```

Lean shapes:

```lean
structure CandidateInvariant (X : Type) where
  holds : X -> Prop
```

or:

```lean
structure NumericalInvariant (X : Type) where
  value : X -> Nat
```

### 6.3 `needToyCheck`

An idea owes a toy check when it has a map and invariant but no finite test.

Toy checks are wind tunnels.

```text
Toy success does not prove reality.
Toy failure can kill bad structure early.
```

Exact preservation:

```lean
def preservesInvariant
  {A B : Type}
  (f : A -> B)
  (IA : A -> Nat)
  (IB : B -> Nat)
  : Prop :=
  forall a : A, IB (f a) = IA a
```

Scaling or ordering preservation:

```lean
def preservesOrdering
  {A B : Type}
  (f : A -> B)
  (IA : A -> Nat)
  (IB : B -> Nat)
  : Prop :=
  forall a1 a2 : A, IA a1 <= IA a2 -> IB (f a1) <= IB (f a2)
```

Exact preservation and scaling preservation must never be confused.

### 6.4 `needNullModel`

An idea owes null-model work when it claims uniqueness, necessity, superiority, or special explanatory status.

A null model asks:

```text
Can a simpler or rival model explain the same thing?
```

Minimal retirement condition, option A:

```text
Supply at least one null model.
```

Minimal retirement condition, option B:

```text
Explicitly disclaim uniqueness, necessity, or superiority.
```

This matters. If an idea does not claim uniqueness, it should not be forced into unnecessary null-model labor. It may retire `needNullModel` by saying:

```text
I do not claim this bridge is necessary or unique. I only claim compatibility worth exploring.
```

Lean shape:

```lean
structure NullModel where
  Carrier : Type
  explains : Prop
```

A null model does not automatically refute an idea. It blocks inflated claims.

### 6.5 `needObstruction`

An idea owes obstruction work when known blockers, no-go results, or contradiction risks apply.

Examples:

```text
chirality obstruction
anomaly obstruction
Lorentz recovery problem
Born rule problem
continuum-limit problem
faithfulness problem
parameter padding
normalization artifact
```

Minimal retirement condition:

```text
State one obstruction, explain whether it applies, and record the consequence.
```

Possible consequences:

```text
obstruction does not apply
idea must be modified
idea fails as stated
idea downgraded to analogy only
idea remains open with known blocker
```

Lean shape:

```lean
structure Obstruction where
  assumptions : Prop
  forbidden : Prop
  noGo : assumptions -> Not forbidden
```

Retiring obstruction debt does not always mean victory. Sometimes it means honest downgrade.

### 6.6 `needFaithfulnessReview`

Faithfulness review asks one question:

```text
Does the formalization match the intended claim?
```

Allowed answers:

```text
yes
no
contested
```

Minimal contested rule:

```text
If contested, the idea remains unpromoted.
The contesting party must state one sentence explaining the mismatch.
The owner may repair, revise, or accept the block.
```

No silent vetoes.

No committee required at the minimum layer.

Faithfulness review matters because Lean can prove the wrong theorem if the definitions are too weak, distorted, or smuggle in the conclusion.

## 7. Final-Truth Claim Handling

The field:

```lean
containsFinalTruthClaim : Bool
```

is not meant to be a punishment mechanism.

It is a promotion blocker.

### 7.1 Owner-Declared, Reviewer-Flaggable, Repairable

The owner should be asked:

```text
Does this claim assert final truth about nature?
```

If yes, promotion is blocked until rephrased.

A tool or reviewer may flag possible final-truth language, but the fix is revision, not punishment.

### 7.2 Examples

Blocked:

```text
This proves the final theory.
This is the true substrate of nature.
This solves quantum gravity.
```

Repairable as:

```text
This is a candidate bridge.
This is a proposed substrate hypothesis.
This is a toy-supported formal obligation.
This is a promoted artifact under current debt conditions.
```

### 7.3 Minimal Rule

```text
Final-truth language does not kill the idea.
It blocks promotion until repaired.
```

## 16. Relationship to Lean

Lean is not the entry gate. Lean is the strongest promotion instrument.

### 16.1 What Lean Is For

Lean helps retire debt by turning claims into:

```text
definitions
structures
inductive types
theorem stubs
proofs
counterexamples
no-go results
executable finite checks
```

### 16.2 What Lean Is Not For

Lean does not prove nature.

Lean does not decide physical faithfulness.

Lean does not replace empirical tests.

Lean does not replace human judgment.

Lean proves only that a formal result follows from formal assumptions.

### 16.3 Lean-First Means Promotion-First, Not Entry-First

Lean-first does not mean every idea must begin in Lean.

It means promoted claims should increasingly have Lean-shaped obligations and, where possible, Lean-checked cores.

### 16.4 The Faithfulness Gate

A Lean theorem can be correct and still irrelevant if it formalizes the wrong claim.

Therefore, every Lean-based debt retirement that supports promotion must face the faithfulness question:

```text
Does the formalization match the intended claim?
```

If contested, promotion waits.

## Quick Note on Method

I'll evaluate each candidate against the EBP 2.1 minimal pipeline:

```
Capture -> Clarify -> Debt -> Test -> Promote
```

Each candidate will get a structured assessment of:
- **Entry legitimacy** (can it enter free?)
- **Default debt owed** (which of the 6 items: needMap, needInvariant, needToyCheck, needNullModel, needObstruction, needFaithfulnessReview)
- **Debt payment status** (which debts are paid, partial, unpaid)
- **Final-truth language present?** (does it claim "this solves it all"?)
- **Promotion verdict** (per EBP 2.1: `ideaPromoted := debt.isEmpty && !containsFinalTruthClaim`)

Then I'll do a final comparative ledger and identify the *next smallest useful move* for each.

## 1. String Theory (M-theory)

### Capture
```
Owner: String community
Claim: Strings in 10/11D, vibrating at different modes, unify all forces and particles.
```

### Debt Status

| Debt Item | Status | Comment |
|---|---|---|
| needMap | **Partial** | The map exists: perturbative string amplitudes -> S-matrix. But the map from "M-theory" to "real-world physics" requires choosing a vacuum among ~10⁵⁰⁰ options — the map is *not* a function, it's a search. |
| needInvariant | **Partial** | Some claimed invariants: conformal invariance on worldsheet, anomaly cancellation conditions, BPS spectra. But the *target* invariants (low-energy SM, cosmological constant) are not derived. |
| needToyCheck | **Partial** | Toy checks exist: AdS/CFT in certain limits, low-dimensional string theory. None reproduces full SM. |
| needNullModel | **Unpaid** | What simpler theory could explain the same? EFTs with the same low-energy behavior. String theory does not make unique low-energy predictions in the real world. |
| needObstruction | **Unpaid (acknowledged)** | Known obstructions: landscape problem, swampland conjectures, no unique vacuum, no experimental signature. These are *filed* but not *retired*. |
| needFaithfulnessReview | **Unpaid** | Many formal results in string theory are mathematically correct but may not faithfully represent "unification of forces." E.g., AdS/CFT is a duality, but is it the "unification" claim? Contested. |

### Final-Truth Language
- **Often present:** "String theory is the leading candidate for a TOE." 
- **Repairable to:** "String theory is a candidate framework for unification, currently without empirical confirmation."

### Promotion Verdict
**Not promotable.** 4 of 6 debts unpaid, 1 partial. Final-truth language present.

### Next Smallest Useful Move
> Pick *one* SM fermion generation and write the explicit map from string spectrum to that generation, including the chirality issue, in one sentence. (Currently, this map requires case-by-case model-building with no general principle.)

## 2. Loop Quantum Gravity (LQG)

### Capture
```
Owner: LQG community
Claim: Spacetime is discrete; spin networks encode geometry; quantizing GR directly gives quantum gravity.
```

### Debt Status

| Debt Item | Status | Comment |
|---|---|---|
| needMap | **Partial** | Map: spin network states -> geometric operators (area, volume). The map is mathematically well-defined. But the map from spin networks to *familiar* spacetime is debated (e.g., the "problem of time" in canonical LQG). |
| needInvariant | **Partial** | Invariant: diffeomorphism invariance, area-gap (smallest non-zero area). The area-gap is a *prediction* in principle, but the experimental signature is unclear. |
| needToyCheck | **Partial** | Toy checks exist: 2+1D gravity, mini-superspace models, some cosmological applications. Full 3+1D recovered spacetime is harder. |
| needNullModel | **Unpaid** | What simpler framework gives the same spin-network phenomenology? Causal set theory, causal dynamical triangulations, and others compete. The claim "this *is* quantum gravity" is uniqueness language, and it's not established. |
| needObstruction | **Partial (acknowledged)** | Known obstructions: classical limit recovery, problem of time, scalar field coupling, graviton propagator. These are *known* but not *resolved*. |
| needFaithfulnessReview | **Partial** | Some worry that "spin foam amplitudes" don't faithfully represent "sum over geometries" in the path-integral sense. Contested. |

### Final-Truth Language
- **Sometimes present:** "LQG is the correct quantization of GR."
- **Repairable to:** "LQG is a candidate quantization of GR with discrete spatial structure."

### Promotion Verdict
**Not promotable.** 2 of 6 debts unpaid, 4 partial. Final-truth language sometimes present.

### Next Smallest Useful Move
> Write one sentence stating the explicit map from one spin network state to a classical spacetime metric in the semi-classical limit, in a specific coherent-state construction. (Currently, this map is technically defined but not concretely demonstrated in the regime relevant to our universe.)

## 3. Causal Set Theory

### Capture
```
Owner: Causal set community
Claim: Spacetime is fundamentally a discrete partial order; the continuum is an approximation.
```

### Debt Status

| Debt Item | Status | Comment |
|---|---|---|
| needMap | **Partial** | Map: causal set -> continuum manifold (via sprinkling). The map is well-defined statistically, but uniqueness is not established. |
| needInvariant | **Paid** | Order, cardinality, dimension. The invariants are well-defined. |
| needToyCheck | **Partial** | Toy checks: 2D causal sets, de Sitter cosmology, scalar field on causal set. SM not reproduced. |
| needNullModel | **Unpaid** | What simpler model gives Lorentz invariance at macroscopic scales? Causal set theory's competitor — emergent spacetime from entanglement — gives similar results with different ontology. |
| needObstruction | **Unpaid (acknowledged)** | The continuum-limit problem: how does a 4D Lorentzian manifold emerge from a discrete order? This is a known and serious obstruction. |
| needFaithfulnessReview | **Partial** | Is the "sprinkling" map faithful to actual physics, or just a mathematical construction? Contested. |

## Cross-Cutting Observations

### 1. The Universal Unpaid Debt: Null Models

Almost every candidate has an *unpaid* `needNullModel`. This is significant. It means that for most TOE candidates, the question "could a simpler theory explain the same data?" is not adequately addressed. This is not a failure of the candidates — it's a feature of the field. Null models are *hard* to construct when you don't have the data.

But under EBP 2.1, this debt is real. A TOE candidate that doesn't retire null-model debt is at risk of being a *Rube Goldberg machine*: elaborate, but not uniquely so.

### 2. The Obstruction Debt Pattern

Most candidates have an *acknowledged but unretired* `needObstruction`. This is actually *good* in a sense: the candidates are aware of their problems. The EBP 2.1 principle is that *acknowledged obstructions* are debt that must be retired, not avoided. So even when a candidate is honest about its problems, that honesty is *not enough* under the protocol — the obstructions must be *addressed* (either by showing they don't apply, or by modifying the claim, or by honestly downgrading).

### 3. The Final-Truth Language Epidemic

String theory, MWI, and LQG all have significant final-truth language in their public presentations. EBP 2.1 treats this as a promotion blocker. Most candidates would need to do *rhetorical repair work* before they could be promoted — even if their technical content were otherwise mature.

This is not a minor issue. EBP 2.1 is explicit: "Final-truth language does not kill the idea. It blocks promotion until repaired." The repair is not a punishment; it's a clarification. But it must be done.

### 4. The "Candidate Artifact" Status

Under EBP 2.1, *no TOE candidate* is currently a "promoted candidate artifact." They are all *alive with debt*. This is not a damning assessment — it's a *realistic* one. The field is in an early stage. EBP 2.1 is designed exactly for this situation: a wide door, a guarded throne, and a clear ledger of what each idea still owes.

The Elephant Bridge Protocol is not a verdict. It's a *tool* for honest accounting. The verdict is: **all TOE candidates are alive; none are promoted; each has its own next smallest useful move.**
