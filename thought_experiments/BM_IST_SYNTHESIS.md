---

### I. Core Mathematics (The Single Attractor Theorem)

**Item 1 — Prove the attractor exists.**  
No one has shown that a realistic effective action on an adelic space actually possesses a unique, compact global attractor with the right fractal properties. This is not a guess; it needs a genuine dynamical systems proof.

**Item 2 — Compute the fractal dimension.**  
The codimension $c$ and Hausdorff dimension $d_H$ of $I_U$ must be derived from the action, not fitted to data. Until this is done, the small parameter $\epsilon$ in the Born-rule deviation is a free fudge factor, not a prediction.

**Item 3 — Show equivariance survives on a fractal.**  
Standard BM proves $|\Psi|^2$ is preserved by the flow using calculus on smooth manifolds. On a fractal attractor, the divergence theorem fails, derivatives are singular, and the standard proof collapses. A generalized equivariance theorem using Hausdorff measure or fractal curvatures is needed.

---

### II. The Adelic Bridge (Connecting Discrete to Continuous)

**Item 4 — Construct the adelic product explicitly.**  
The claim that the archimedean place emerges from an adelic product over all primes is formal. No one has written down the restricted product for a realistic many-body system and shown it converges to standard Schrödinger quantum mechanics in the macroscopic limit.

**Item 5 — Derive the archimedean gravitational term.**  
The assertion that gravity appears as the self-consistency condition for the archimedean component is a sketch. A concrete calculation showing how the adelic product modifies the Einstein field equations at Planck scale is missing.

**Item 6 — Handle the measure-zero problem.**  
The fractal attractor has measure zero in the ambient adelic space. Standard probability theory assigns zero probability to measure-zero sets. You need a new probability framework (likely based on the SRB measure) that treats the attractor as the *only* physically relevant set, not as an impossible coincidence.

---

### III. Dynamics & Equations of Motion

**Item 7 — Replace the guiding equation with an EA EOM.**  
The effective action equations of motion proposed to replace the Bohmian velocity law have only been studied in toy models. For interacting many-particle systems, it is unknown whether they reproduce standard quantum statistics or develop pathological instabilities.

**Item 8 — Unify continuous and symbolic dynamics.**  
IST uses discrete bit-string shifts; BM uses smooth differential equations. A single Lagrangian or Hamiltonian that governs both regimes simultaneously does not exist. Without it, the theory has two clocks and no clear time parameter.

**Item 9 — Define time on the attractor.**  
If the invariant set is atemporal, as IST claims, then the temporal evolution of particle trajectories must be reconstructed from it. No one has shown how to derive a sensible $t$-parameter from a timeless fractal geometry.

---

### IV. Quantum Field Theory

**Item 10 — Define the fractal measure on Fock space.**  
The Radon-Nikodym density $\rho_{\text{frac}}$ for jump rates was written as a formula, but Fock space is infinite-dimensional. Defining a Hausdorff measure on an infinite-dimensional fractal is technically wide open.

**Item 11 — Handle particle creation rigorously.**  
The DGTZ jump-rate modification assumes a particle-number basis. For gauge theories and relativistic fields, particle number is not conserved and the basis is not well-defined. The modification has not been extended to QED or the Standard Model.

**Item 12 — Prove Lorentz covariance of the nonlocal law.**  
BM requires a preferred foliation of spacetime. The synthesis inherits this problem. Showing that the adelic structure picks out a preferred frame *dynamically* rather than by fiat is unsolved.

---

### V. Gravity & Cosmology

**Item 13 — Make PFQG interact with the attractor.**  
Piecewise flat quantum gravity is a proposal, not an established framework. The claim that the fractal measure replaces the Wheeler-DeWitt measure has never been calculated explicitly in a triangulated model.

**Item 14 — Compute the cosmological constant from geometry.**  
If the archimedean shadow of $I_U$ modifies the Einstein equations, the theory should predict the cosmological constant $\Lambda$. No such calculation exists.

**Item 15 — Solve the initial-value problem globally.**  
The atemporal attractor constraint turns BM from an initial-value theory into a boundary-value problem for the entire history of the universe. No algorithm or existence theorem for such global solutions is known.

---

### VI. Phenomenology & Testability

**Item 16 — Quantify the irrational-angle effect.**  
The prediction of Born-rule deviations at irrational angles is qualitative. A concrete calculation giving the amplitude $\epsilon$ as a function of the attractor’s fractal dimension is needed before anyone can design an experiment.

**Item 17 — Identify the mesoscopic ceiling scale.**  
The superposition size limit $N_c$ depends on the codimension of $I_U$ in configuration space. Without computing that codimension, the theory cannot say whether the ceiling is at $10^4$, $10^{10}$, or $10^{30}$ amu.

**Item 18 — Distinguish geometric exclusion from environmental decoherence.**  
The theory claims its ceiling is environment-independent, but real experiments always have some environment. A quantitative calculation showing how geometric exclusion dominates over residual environmental effects at the predicted scale is missing.

---

### VII. Conceptual & Foundational

**Item 19 — Answer the conspiracy objection rigorously.**  
Wood & Spekkens’ critique is answered only if the attractor constraint is shown to be a *necessary* consequence of the dynamics, not a hand-picked initial condition. A theorem proving this necessity is still absent.

**Item 20 — Reconcile the wave function’s dual status.**  
The synthesis claims $\Psi$ is both emergent (from the fractal trajectories) and nomological (dynamically efficacious). These two roles pull in opposite directions. A precise account of how a coarse-grained statistical object can simultaneously act as a law of motion is still lacking.
