# Where Geometry Shines in Physics: Spacetime, Forces, and Fiber Bundles

## The organizing idea
Geometry does not shine in physics as a discrete counter, a statistical accountant, or a rigid algebraic constraint. It shines in three specific physical structures: **spacetime/kinematics, dynamics/forces, and gauge symmetries**. Not all motion, not all forces, not all symmetries — but a specific, identifiable subclass.

The dividing line is this: geometry is present when the physical structure involves continuous extension, curvature, distance, or parallel transport. Without a manifold, a metric, or a connection, geometry is absent. With it, geometry is not just present but often the most fundamental way to describe the interaction.

Below: the three structures, the geometric principle in each, and the class of physical systems where geometry is genuinely active.

### 1. Spacetime and Kinematics (The Arena and the Paths)
**What it is**
Spacetime is the manifold on which physics happens; kinematics is the study of allowed trajectories (geodesics) within it. 

**When does a structure have geometric content?**
A generic background is just a set of coordinates. It has no geometric structure beyond its dimensionality. A structure has geometric content when it possesses a metric (defining distance and causal structure) or a connection (defining parallel transport).

**Four levels of geometric content in kinematics:**
*   **Level 1 — Flat/Euclidean.** The metric is trivial. This appears in: Newtonian mechanics, non-relativistic quantum mechanics.
*   **Level 2 — Pseudo-Riemannian.** The metric has a Lorentzian signature, defining light cones and causal structure. This appears in: Special and General Relativity.
*   **Level 3 — Fiber Bundles.** The space has an "internal" geometry attached to every point in spacetime. This appears in: Kaluza-Klein theory, internal state spaces.
*   **Level 4 — Non-commutative/Quantum Geometry.** The coordinates themselves fail to commute, replacing points with algebras. This appears in: String theory, Loop Quantum Gravity.

**The duality**
The duality is between the physical trajectory and the geometric shortest path.
| Physical concept | Geometric object | Duality |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free fall / Inertia | Geodesic | Extremal proper time |
| Light propagation | Null geodesic | Causal cone boundary |
| Particle spin | Spin connection | Parallel transport of frames |
| Aharonov-Bohm effect | Holonomy | Phase from path-dependent transport |

**Where geometry shines**
*   **Strongest case: General Relativity.** Gravity is not a force; it is the curvature of spacetime. The Einstein field equations are purely geometric. This is geometry as the fundamental physical interaction.
*   **Second strongest: Causal structure.** The light cone dictates what can influence what. The geometry of the metric strictly enforces causality.
*   **Third: Geodesic motion.** The principle of least action in mechanics is mathematically identical to finding the shortest path in a curved space.

**Where geometry is not active**
*   Purely internal quantum state spaces (Hilbert space is linear; its "geometry" is a derived metric, not a fundamental arena).
*   Thermodynamic phase spaces (driven by entropy and probability, not spatial distance).

### 2. Dynamics and Forces (The Dance and the Choreography)
**What it is**
Dynamics dictates how systems evolve. In modern physics, forces are not pushes and pulls; they are the curvature of connections on fiber bundles.

**When does a force have geometric content?**
A generic force is a vector field. It has no inherent geometric structure. A force has geometric content when it is identified with the curvature tensor of a gauge connection.

**Four types of geometric dynamics:**
*   **Type 1 — Metric curvature.** Gravity. The Riemann curvature tensor of the Levi-Civita connection.
*   **Type 2 — Gauge curvature.** Electromagnetism, Strong, and Weak forces. The field strength tensor $F_{\mu\nu}$ is the curvature of a $U(1)$, $SU(2)$, or $SU(3)$ connection.
*   **Type 3 — Symplectic geometry.** The phase space of classical mechanics is a symplectic manifold. Hamilton's equations are geometric flows.
*   **Type 4 — Topological dynamics.** Instantons and monopoles. The dynamics are governed by the topological winding of the geometric fields.

**The duality**
The duality is between the physical force and the geometric curvature.
| Physical force | Geometric object | Structure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gravity | Riemann curvature | Metric connection |
| Electromagnetism | $U(1)$ curvature | Principal bundle |
| Strong/Weak force | $SU(3)/SU(2)$ curvature | Yang-Mills connection |
| Berry phase | Holonomy of parameter space | Fiber bundle over control space |

**Where geometry shines**
*   **Strongest case: The Standard Model.** All fundamental forces (except gravity) are Yang-Mills gauge theories. The dynamics of quarks and leptons are entirely dictated by the geometry of $SU(3) \times SU(2) \times U(1)$ principal bundles.
*   **Second strongest: Geometric Quantization.** The transition from classical to quantum mechanics is governed by the symplectic geometry of phase space (prequantum line bundles, polarization).
*   **Third: Condensed matter topology.** The quantum Hall effect and topological insulators are governed by the geometric Berry curvature of the Bloch bands.

### 3. Symmetries and Fiber Bundles (The Rules and the Conserved Quantities)
**What it is**
Symmetries are transformations that leave the geometric structure invariant. In physics, they form Lie groups, and their actions are organized by fiber bundles.

**When does a symmetry have geometric content?**
A generic symmetry is a discrete permutation. It has no geometric content. A symmetry has geometric content when it is a continuous Lie group acting smoothly on a manifold or a fiber bundle (diffeomorphisms, gauge transformations).

**Four types of geometric symmetry:**
*   **Type 1 — Isometries.** Transformations that preserve the metric (Poincaré group).
*   **Type 2 — Diffeomorphisms.** Transformations that preserve the smooth structure of the manifold (General covariance).
*   **Type 3 — Gauge transformations.** Automorphisms of a fiber bundle that preserve the connection.
*   **Type 4 — Geometric dualities.** Equivalences between different geometric descriptions (T-duality, Mirror symmetry).

**The duality**
The duality is between the physical conservation law and the geometric symmetry (Noether's theorem).
| Physical symmetry | Geometric object | Duality |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Energy/Momentum conservation | Spacetime isometries (Killing vectors) | Noether's theorem |
| Charge conservation | Gauge symmetry (Fiber bundle automorphism) | Covariant derivative |
| Equivalence principle | Diffeomorphism invariance | General covariance |
| Mirror symmetry | Calabi-Yau moduli space | Equivalence of A/B models |

**Where geometry shines**
*   **Strongest case: Noether's Theorem.** Every continuous geometric symmetry of the action yields a conserved physical quantity. This is geometry as the ultimate source of physical conservation laws.
*   **Second strongest: Gauge principle.** Requiring local geometric symmetry (gauge invariance) *forces* the existence of interaction fields (photons, gluons). Geometry dictates what forces must exist.
*   **Third: String dualities.** T-duality and mirror symmetry show that radically different geometries yield identical physics. Geometry is revealed to be a descriptive choice, not an absolute reality.

### 4. Where Spacetime, Dynamics, and Symmetries meet
The deepest case for geometry in physics is when spacetime, dynamics, and symmetries all have geometric content simultaneously. 

**Three examples:**
1.  **General Relativity:** 
    *   *Spacetime:* Lorentzian manifold.
    *   *Dynamics:* Einstein-Hilbert action (scalar curvature).
    *   *Symmetry:* Diffeomorphism invariance.
    *   *Geometric structure:* Complete.
2.  **Yang-Mills Theory (Standard Model):**
    *   *Spacetime:* Base manifold.
    *   *Dynamics:* Yang-Mills action (trace of curvature squared).
    *   *Symmetry:* Local gauge transformations.
    *   *Geometric structure:* Complete.
3.  **String Theory Compactifications:**
    *   *Spacetime:* Calabi-Yau manifold.
    *   *Dynamics:* Worldsheet sigma model.
    *   *Symmetry:* Mirror symmetry / Modular invariance.
    *   *Geometric structure:* Complete.

### 5. The dividing line
The pattern across all three structures is:
**Geometry is active when:**
*   The system involves continuous extension, metrics, or distances.
*   The force is mediated by a connection or curvature.
*   The symmetry is a continuous Lie group acting on a manifold.

**Geometry is a language when:**
*   The system is discrete (lattice models, spin chains).
*   The "forces" are effective, statistical, or entropic.
*   The symmetries are discrete (crystallographic, C, P, T).

**Geometry is inapplicable when:**
*   The system is purely algebraic or arithmetic (discrete spectra, number theory).
*   The dynamics are driven by probability, entropy, or measurement collapse.
*   The structure is fundamentally non-commutative at the Planck scale (where the manifold concept breaks down).

### 6. The one-sentence summary
Geometry shines in physics at the level of spacetime, forces, and gauge symmetries when the structures involved possess continuous extension, curvature, and parallel transport — as in General Relativity, the Standard Model, and String Theory — and it is a language rather than a cause when the structures are discrete or statistical, and it is inapplicable when the structures are purely algebraic or entropic.

It partially constrains the nature of quantum gravity and the emergence of spacetime, but only at the boundary where the smooth manifold concept begins to fracture and the underlying quantum information begins to show through. The nine-round program mapped these boundaries with the precision of a topographic survey — and the map shows that geometry is not the substance of physics, not the calculator of physics, and not the oracle of physics. It is the architecture of physics: the continuous, curved, and symmetric framework that holds the stage together — enforced in the bulk, silent at the singularities, and testable wherever the curvature is sharp enough to reveal it.

# Where Geometry Shines in Physics: Curvature, Phase Space, Moduli

*A companion map to the arithmetic survey — same method: identify the structures, find the dividing line, rank the cases, and mark the silences.*

---

## The organizing idea

Geometry does not shine in physics as visualization, as intuition, or as a notation for what the algebra already says. Diagrams are not geometry. Embedding pictures are not geometry. $\mathbb{R}^n$ scaffolding is not geometry. Geometry shines in **three specific mathematical structures**: **curvature** (force), **phase space** (motion), and **moduli** (classification). Not all uses of space, not every manifold, not every metric — a specific, identifiable subclass.

The dividing line is this: **geometry is present when the physics *is* the structure of a space — when the observables are geometric invariants (holonomy, curvature, geodesic distance, area, period) and deforming the space changes the physics.** Without that identification, geometry is a language. With it, geometry is not merely present; it is often the only formulation the law admits.

And one asymmetry with the companion map: arithmetic in physics is always constraint, never engine. Geometry is frequently the engine — gravity *is* curvature pushing back — which is why this map has a section the arithmetic map lacks: geometry as output, not just input.

## 1. Curvature (force)

### What it is here

A connection on a bundle; its curvature; holonomy around loops. In physics: gravitational field = curvature of spacetime; gauge field = curvature of a principal bundle; Berry phase = holonomy of an eigenbundle over parameter space.

### Four levels of geometric content

**Level 1 — Topological.** Only the homotopy class matters. Dirac quantization $eg = 2\pi n\hbar$; anyonic braiding; Chern classes.

**Level 2 — Holonomy.** Path-dependent phases: Aharonov–Bohm, Berry/Wilson loops. Measurable as phase shifts.

**Level 3 — Curvature proper.** Yang–Mills field strength; Riemann tensor; geodesic deviation.

**Level 4 — Spectral/global.** Shape known only through spectra (inverse problems) or through boundary data (holography).

### The identification

| Physical structure | Geometric object | Identification |
|---|---|---|
| Gravity | Lorentzian metric | Free fall = geodesic; field = curvature |
| Gauge force | Connection on a bundle | Field strength = curvature; charge = holonomy |
| Berry/A–B phase | Eigenbundle holonomy | Phase = geometry of parameter space |
| Anyon statistics | Braid group of configuration space | Statistics = homotopy |
| Anomaly | Index $\int \hat{A} \wedge \operatorname{ch}(E)$ | Consistency = a curvature integral vanishing |

### Where geometry shines

**Strongest case: general relativity.** Curvature is measured — light bending, perihelion precession, Shapiro delay, gravitational-wave phase, GPS time dilation. Geometry is not the language of gravity; it **is** gravity. Geodesic motion is not analogous to straight lines; it is the straightest possible in the metric, and the metric is the field.

**Second: gauge theory.** Every non-gravitational force is a connection. The Standard Model is a choice of bundle and connection; anomaly cancellation is the vanishing of a geometric index; instantons are classified by bundle topology. There is no formulation that removes the geometry without removing the theory.

**Third: holonomy phases.** The Aharonov–Bohm phase depends on the loop's homotopy class, not on local forces. The Berry phase is measured interferometrically and exploited in molecular dynamics and topological band theory. Geometry observable in a phase shift.

**Fourth: topological order.** Ground-state degeneracy on a torus; anyon sectors. The *topology of space* is a physical observable: change the manifold, change the degeneracy.

### Where geometry is not active

- Free particle on $\mathbb{R}^n$: **continuous ≠ geometric.** The continuum is present; no curvature, no holonomy, no metric doing work.
- Quantum numbers from internal symmetry (SO(4) in hydrogen): group structure, not space structure.
- Feynman diagrams: spatial *pictures* of algebraic objects.

## 2. Phase space (motion)

### What it is here

A state space with structure: symplectic (classical), projective (quantum, partially). Dynamics = flow; trajectories = geodesics after reduction; conservation = geometry; integrability = fibration.

### Four levels

**Level 1 — Bare configuration space.** Paths, but no structure doing work.

**Level 2 — Symplectic.** Hamiltonian flow, Noether's theorem, symplectic reduction. The standard classical world.

**Level 3 — Metricized motion.** Maupertuis–Jacobi: trajectories = geodesics of the Jacobi metric. KAM tori; integrability as torus fibration (Liouville–Arnold).

**Level 4 — Quantum state geometry.** Fubini–Study metric; geometric phases; quantum speed limits.

### The identification

| Physical structure | Geometric object | Identification |
|---|---|---|
| Classical motion | Hamiltonian flow on symplectic $M$ | Dynamics = flow on the space |
| Conserved quantity | Moment map | Symmetry = geometry of the action |
| Integrable system | Invariant tori / spectral curve | Solvability = geometry of the fibration |
| Adiabatic transport | Berry connection | Memory = holonomy |
| Quantum evolution | Fubini–Study geometry of $\mathbb{P}(\mathcal{H})$ | Speed limit = FS distance / energy spread |

### Where geometry shines

**Strongest case: Hamiltonian mechanics.** Noether's theorem, canonical transformations, reduction, KAM theory — the entire edifice is the geometry of phase space. Classical mechanics is not *formulable* in geometry; it **is** symplectic geometry with extra steps.

**Second: integrability.** Liouville–Arnold: integrable = fibred by invariant tori. Lax pairs, inverse scattering, solitons — solvability is a geometric statement about the foliation of the state space.

**Third: adiabatic phases.** A system cycled around parameter space returns with a phase no local force can supply — the holonomy of the eigenbundle. Measured, and the basis of geometric-gate proposals in quantum computing.

**Fourth (partial): quantum kinematics.** Projective Hilbert space carries a genuine metric, and it does work: the Mandelstam–Tamm speed limit makes the fastest possible evolution a Fubini–Study distance per unit energy spread. But the modulus — the Born statistics — is not FS geometry. Geometry governs the phases; it is silent about the probabilities. (Boundary case, §7.)

### Where geometry is not active

- The Born rule: no shape implies $|\psi|^2$.
- Measurement and collapse: no geometry has ever proposed a collapse mechanism that was not smuggled-in dynamics.
- Superposition: the linear algebra is prior; the manifold description inherits from it, not vice versa.

## 3. Moduli (classification)

### What it is here

The space of solutions/vacua/theories, as a geometric object. Its shape *is* the physics: singularities = phase transitions; strata = order parameters; monodromy = duality.

### Four levels

**Level 1 — Discrete classification by invariants.** (Where the arithmetic document takes over: Chern numbers, anyon data, Galois action.)

**Level 2 — Continuous moduli.** Coupling spaces, conformal manifolds, exactly marginal directions.

**Level 3 — Moduli with singularities.** Seiberg–Witten vacuum structure; physics read off the periods of a curve.

**Level 4 — Theory space with dualities.** Mirror symmetry; S/T/U duality webs; the geometry of the space of theories.

### The identification

| Physical structure | Geometric object | Identification |
|---|---|---|
| $\mathcal{N}=2$ SYM vacua | Coulomb branch + SW curve | Spectrum = periods; duality = monodromy |
| A/B-models | Mirror Calabi–Yau pair | Physical equivalence = geometric mirror |
| Smooth 4-manifold invariants | Donaldson/SW invariants | QFT computes pure topology |
| CFT consistency | Moduli of Riemann surfaces | Torus mapping class group = $SL(2,\mathbb{Z})$ |
| Holography | RG flow ↔ extra dimension | Scale = geometry |

### Where geometry shines

**Strongest case: Seiberg–Witten.** The low-energy effective theory of $\mathcal{N}=2$ Yang–Mills is *read off* the geometry of its vacuum space: BPS masses = periods of an elliptic curve, electric-magnetic duality = monodromy around singular points. The physics was extracted from the shape. (And note the handoff: the curve is elliptic — its periods are arithmetic objects. The two maps meet exactly here.)

**Second: mirror symmetry.** A physical equivalence between A- and B-models became an enumerative machine: the count of degree-3 rational curves on the quintic threefold — **317,206,375** — was predicted by mirror-map computation before algebraic geometry could reach it. Physics solving geometry, then geometry repaying the debt.

**Third: topology via QFT.** Donaldson and Seiberg–Witten invariants: physical path integrals probing smooth structures that no elementary topological method reaches. The traffic runs both directions.

**Fourth: modular invariance as geometry.** $SL(2,\mathbb{Z})$ is the mapping class group of the torus; torus consistency is a statement about worldsheet geometry. But what the modular group acts *on* — conformal weights, quantum dimensions, characters — is arithmetic. Geometry selects the group; arithmetic supplies the data.

### Where geometry is not active

- The arithmetic fine structure of spectra: exact algebraic values, Galois action.
- Which moduli problem: gauge group, dimension, matter content are inputs to the moduli space, never outputs.
- The landscape: geometry maps the terrain; it selects no address.

## 4. The frontier: geometry as output

Everything above treats geometry as input — a space given, physics identified with its structure. The holographic program inverts this.

- **Ryu–Takayanagi:** boundary entanglement entropy = area of an extremal bulk surface. A theorem within AdS/CFT. The deepest known identification: **entanglement = geometry.**
- **Modular flow:** the Tomita–Takesaki modular flow of the boundary algebra acts as bulk time in the entanglement wedge. Boundary algebra → bulk dynamics.
- **"It from qubit":** the program that bulk geometry *is* boundary entanglement structure.

Status: **PARTIAL.** RT is a theorem; full reconstruction of local bulk physics from boundary data is a program, not a derivation. If it completes, geometry exits the "input" column entirely — not the stage, not the engine, but the *output*. This is the one place where the two maps point the same way from opposite ends: arithmetic was never the substance of the world, and geometry may not be either. Status: OPEN.

## 5. Where they meet

The deepest cases have all three structures interlocked — and interlocked with the arithmetic map.

**Example 1 — General relativity.** Force = curvature (§1); dynamics = flow on superspace, the space of 3-geometries (§2); solution space and gravitational instantons = moduli (§3). All three, intrinsically.

**Example 2 — 4D Yang–Mills.** Force = connection curvature; dynamics = symplectic flow on the space of connections; classification = instanton moduli — which reversed into Donaldson theory. Physics discovered geometry; geometry repaid in kind.

**Example 3 — Worldsheet string theory.** Target-space geometry ↔ worldsheet conformal geometry ↔ moduli of Riemann surfaces, with the modular group acting on arithmetic spectra. The precise hinge between the two documents.

**The geometry–arithmetic interface (five hinges):**

1. **Index theorem (Atiyah–Singer):** anomaly cancellation = a curvature integral = a topological integer. Geometry supplies the curvature; arithmetic counts it. Anomalies are where shape and number agree to forbid.
2. **Trace formulas (Selberg):** spectrum ↔ closed geodesics. On arithmetic surfaces, lengths are logarithms of algebraic numbers — geometry handing its spectrum to arithmetic.
3. **Anyons:** the braid group is configuration-space topology — geometry picks the group; braiding phases are roots of unity — arithmetic picks the phases. Geometry without arithmetic is a group without its characters.
4. **Dirac quantization:** the integer comes from the topology of a U(1) bundle — geometry. The companion map already ruled: integers from topology are arithmetic-as-language. The handoff is clean.
5. **Crystals:** the lattice is geometry (space groups); the restriction to $n \in \{1,2,3,4,6\}$ is arithmetic (integral quadratic forms). Both maps claim their half.

## 6. The dividing line

**Geometry is active when:**

- The force is a connection/curvature.
- The motion is a geodesic/flow on a structured state space.
- The classification is the shape of a moduli space.
- The observables are geometric invariants: holonomy, Chern class, period, area.

**Geometry is a language when:**

- The space is a backdrop with trivial structure ($\mathbb{R}^n$ kinematics).
- Information geometry, contact thermodynamics: real structure, genuine theorems, but the physics is not *in* the shape.
- Diagrams and embeddings illustrating algebraic facts.

**Geometry is silent when:**

- The question is a quantum postulate: Born rule, measurement, $\hbar$.
- The question is a parameter: dimension, signature, gauge group, particle content, cosmological constant.
- The question is arithmetic fine structure: exact algebraic spectra, Galois action.
- The question is probability as such, and the thermodynamic arrow.

The dividing line is **whether shape does physical work.** If deforming the space changes observables, and the observables are geometric invariants, geometry is active. If the space is scaffolding whose shape is irrelevant to the content, geometry is language. If the essential structure is not spatial at all — algebraic, probabilistic, legislative — geometry is silent.

Three distinctions carry the weight:

- **Continuous ≠ geometric** (free particle on $\mathbb{R}$).
- **Spatial ≠ geometric** (Feynman diagrams, wormhole embeddings).
- **Curved ≠ doing work** (a curved embedding of a flat-physics problem is decoration).

## 7. Capacities and limits

### ✅ Five capacities

1. **Force as curvature** — gravity, gauge fields, Berry holonomy. Strongest: GR, tested.
2. **Motion as geodesy** — symplectic mechanics, Noether, KAM, integrability.
3. **Constraint as topology** — Dirac quantization, anomaly cancellation, topological order.
4. **Classification as moduli** — Seiberg–Witten, mirror symmetry, Donaldson.
5. **Emergence as holography** — RT formula, modular flow. Partial.

### ❌ Four silences

1. **Geometry does not generate the quantum.** The Born rule, measurement, superposition's linearity — none is a statement about any space's shape. Deformation quantization deforms the *algebra*; geometry is what gets lost in quantization, partially recovered (Fubini–Study) but never sovereign.
2. **Geometry does not select its own parameters.** Dimension, signature, gauge group, content: inputs. Kaluza–Klein shows forces *can* be geometry in higher dimensions; it does not say why 3+1 are large. The choice of manifold is where geometry stops and experiment begins.
3. **Geometry does not fix arithmetic fine structure.** Isospectral, non-isometric (Gordon–Webb–Wolpert): different shapes, identical sounds — the spectrum does not determine the shape. And generically the eigenvalues are transcendental mush — the shape does not determine an exact arithmetic spectrum either. Worse, in quantum chaos the Wigner–Dyson universality *erases* geometry: Sinai and stadium billiards share statistics; shape-specific information is discarded in the universal regime. Where geometry gives out, statistics and arithmetic take over — precisely the slot the companion map's "arithmetic quantum chaos" occupies.
4. **Geometry does not legislate probability or the arrow.** Ensembles, entropy increase, irreversibility: the shape of phase space is the same before and after coarse-graining. Geometry is the arena of the arrow; it is not the arrow.

### ⚠️ Three boundary cases

1. **Quantum state space.** Fubini–Study geometry is real and physical (geometric phases, speed limits) but governs phases, not statistics. Geometry is active on the phase circle of the state space and silent at its modulus. Status: PARTIALLY APPLICABLE.
2. **Emergent spacetime.** RT is a theorem; it-from-qubit is a program. Status: PARTIALLY APPLICABLE — the sharpest open question in the subject.
3. **Noncommutative geometry.** Connes' spectral triples as geometry for quantum algebras, with the Standard Model as an application: powerful as language; the parameters remain inputs. Substance or notation: UNRESOLVED.

## 9. The one-sentence summary

**Geometry shines in physics at the level of curvature, phase space, and moduli — when forces are connections, motion is geodesy, and classification is the shape of solution spaces, as in general relativity, gauge theory, and mirror symmetry — and it is a language rather than a substance when the space is a backdrop or a bare state space, and it is silent when the question is a quantum postulate, a parameter selection, or an arithmetic fine structure.**

And the one-paragraph version, mirroring the companion:

> **Geometry in physics is the stage that sometimes becomes the engine — never the script, never the casting director. It shines brightest when physics demands an identification: which curvature, which holonomy, which geodesic, which period — and there it is not a description of the physics but the physics itself, as in gravity, gauge force, and the vacuum spaces of supersymmetric theory. It is silent when physics asks why this dimension, why this gauge group, why these probabilities, why these spectra down to their algebraic digits — questions answered by postulate, by experiment, or by the arithmetic of the companion map. It partially governs the quantum state space — it fixes the phases and the speed limits, but not the statistics — and it may, if holography completes, turn out to be the output rather than the input. The two maps are mirror images: arithmetic is the constitution at the sharp corners, geometry is the substance in the bulk, and they meet at the index theorem and the modular torus — where curvature is counted, and numbers are shaped.**
