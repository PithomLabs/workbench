**The Occam Strategy:**
1. **Do not replace the engine.** Keep Calculus (RG flow) and Geometry (GR) as the primary drivers. 
2. **Identify the ad-hoc input.** Where does the null model require a "fudge factor" (e.g., momentum cutoff $\Lambda$, arbitrary coupling constants)?
3. **Apply the minimal mathematical constraint.** Can *Topology* (invariants) or *Arithmetic* (Tate product formula) naturally forbid the divergence or fix the parameter *without* introducing new particles or dimensions?
4. **Derive the discriminator.** If the arithmetic/topological constraint is real, it must forbid a physical process that the standard null model allows (or vice versa).
