## PART I: The Epistemological Firewall (The 4 Axioms)

**Axiom 1: Map vs. Territory (The Language Rule)**
Mathematics is the space of all logically consistent structures. Physics is the single empirically selected structure. Mathematics is the *language* of physics, not its *substance*. 
*   *Agent Directive:* Never claim that a mathematical structure (e.g., Adeles, Tate curves, Motives) "is" the physical universe. It is only a dictionary used to translate specific physical invariants.

**Axiom 2: Generator vs. Invariant (The Causality Rule)**
Physics consists of things that *happen* (Generators/Dynamics) and things that *remain true* (Invariants/Classifications). 
*   *Agent Directive:* Never attempt to use an invariant (like a topological number, an arithmetic height, or a modular form) to generate a dynamic flow (like the Schrödinger equation or the Einstein field equations). Invariants constrain; they do not choreograph.

**Axiom 3: The Silence Rule (The Jurisdiction Matrix)**
Every mathematical language has a domain where it shines, and a domain where it is constitutionally silent. 
*   *Agent Directive:* Before applying a mathematical tool, consult the Jurisdiction Matrix (Part II). If the physical question falls into the "Silent" category for that math branch, the inquiry is immediately killed.

**Axiom 4: The Null-Separation Guillotine (The Occam Rule)**
A mathematical structure is only physically relevant if removing it changes the physical prediction. If a generic, simpler mathematical null (e.g., standard topology, continuous geometry) reproduces the exact same physical effect, the complex structure is a **Spectator**.
*   *Agent Directive:* Never promote a mathematical correspondence to a physical coupling without passing the Ablation Test.

*   Does the proposal attempt to *generate* dynamics from an invariant? -> **KILL.**

**Step 3: The Null-Separation Test**
*   Identify the physical observable being claimed (e.g., "Arithmetic dictates the Hall conductance").

**Step 4: The Invariant Translation**
*   Can the surviving arithmetic structure be mapped to a known physical invariant (e.g., a Period, a Modular Form, an Index Theorem) without adding free parameters?
    *   If YES -> **PROMOTE to B1/B2 Invariant Dictionary.**
