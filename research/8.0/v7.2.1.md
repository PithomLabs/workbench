# 0. Executive Thesis

The previous architecture began from a strong assumption:

> A discrete/arithmetic microscopic dynamical system should generate the continuum quantum world through a sequence of microscopic dynamics, coarse-graining, projection, amplitude emergence, and RG flow.

Adversarial work showed that this formulation was valuable as a stress test but too narrow as a statement of what the final theory must look like.

The v7.2 program therefore does **not** make an IST-style invariant set or any specific microscopic substrate the foundation. It instead asks whether a Tate/adelic mathematical structure can be integrated with an experimentally successful Bohmian quantum theory and whether that integration yields a new, null-separating physical consequence.

The foundational question is:

$$
\boxed{
\text{What is the smallest common mathematical structure in which
continuum BM physics and arithmetic/adelic structure can coexist,
constrain one another, and yield testable consequences without either
being silently identified with the other?}
}
$$

The revised hypothesis has four structural commitments, none yet promoted to established physics:

1. **Adelic/local-global possibility:** real and non-Archimedean sectors are mathematically distinct local sectors that may participate in one global compatibility structure.
2. **Arithmetic admissibility:** arithmetic may constrain the joint physical state/observable structure, but it is not assumed to generate the continuum or to shrink ordinary BM configuration space by fiat.
3. **BM-first physical anchor:** ordinary Bohmian Mechanics supplies the IR physical baseline—wavefunction, actual configuration, guidance dynamics, and quantum-equilibrium/observational benchmark—against which any adelic extension is compared.
4. **Scale closure:** the resulting effective theory must possess controlled scale dependence; Asymptotic Safety is a candidate UV/RG organizing principle, not the UV ontology itself.

The synthesis is therefore:

$$
\boxed{
\text{Tate/adelic mathematics}
\;+
\text{BM IR physical baseline}
\;+
\text{explicit typed bridge}
\;+
\text{AS UV/RG closure}
}
$$

The ultraviolet representation remains open. A spectral/noncommutative-geometric structure coupled to adelic data is a candidate, not an axiom.

Operationally:

$$

The arrows remain theorem targets, not facts.

# 1. The GR-Refines-Newton Lesson, Recast

General relativity did not become deeper merely by adding more equations to Newtonian mechanics.

Its strength came from changing the underlying conceptual structure while recovering Newton in the regime where Newton had already succeeded.

The research program now adopts the same methodological lesson:

1. **Preserve successful phenomenology.**
2. **Remove only assumptions that are not forced by observation or theorem.**
3. **Replace those assumptions with a broader structure rather than a new collection of special-case mechanisms.**
4. **Produce a new consequence that the older description did not require.**

Our problem is different from Newton/GR in one critical respect:

**we do not yet have a Mercury.**

We have candidate exact-discrete corners, candidate arithmetic selection mechanisms, and candidate Bell/information-capacity channels. None is allowed to be called an anomaly until a quantitative residual or selection rule is actually derived.

The required refinement is therefore:

$$
\boxed{
\text{successful continuum QM}
+
\text{new arithmetic structure}
=
\text{testable refinement}
}
$$

not:

$$
\text{successful continuum QM}
=
\text{evidence that arithmetic must already be there}.
$$

# 5. The Adelic Principle

## 5.1 Mathematical starting point

The rational field has an Archimedean completion and non-Archimedean completions:

$$
\mathbb Q\to\mathbb R,
\qquad
\mathbb Q\to\mathbb Q_p.
$$

The adele ring is

$$
\mathbb A_{\mathbb Q}
=
\mathbb R
\times
\prod_p' \mathbb Q_p.
$$

The restricted product is the standard number-theoretic structure. The individual $\mathbb Q_p$ are not discrete topological spaces; finite quotients such as

$$
\mathbb Z_p/p^L\mathbb Z_p
$$

are finite discrete approximants.

v7 therefore uses:

$$
\boxed{
\text{Archimedean}
\leftrightarrow
\text{non-Archimedean}
}
$$

as a local/completion distinction, not as "continuous versus discrete" in a literal topological sense.

## 5.2 Physical hypothesis

The program permits, but does not assume, that physical data admit local descriptions

$$
\mathfrak U_v,
\qquad
v=\infty,2,3,5,\ldots
$$

with a globally admissible subset

$$
\mathfrak U_{\rm global}
\subseteq
\prod_v' \mathfrak U_v.
$$

The global condition is the physical candidate.

The existence of local sectors by itself is not the physical claim.

## 5.3 No automatic Hasse principle

A Hasse-principle analogy is allowed only where the actual mathematical problem has the required local-global theorem.

The program therefore uses the neutral phrase:

$$
\boxed{\text{adelic local-global compatibility}}
$$

and introduces Hasse principles, strong approximation, reciprocity, or adelic harmonic analysis only when their hypotheses actually apply.

## 5.4 Global character structure

Adelic harmonic analysis supplies a mathematically precise precedent for global/local character factorization.

Schematically:

$$
\chi_{\mathbb A}(x)
=
\chi_\infty(x_\infty)
\prod_p\chi_p(x_p).
$$

This is useful because the candidate arithmetic phase sector can be formulated through characters rather than through an analogy between a p-adic label and a complex phase.

# 5A. The Freund–Witten Adelic Amplitude Benchmark

## 5A.1 Why this benchmark is admitted

v7.0 already permits a compatibility bridge of the form

$$
C_{\rm global}(\omega_\infty,\omega_2,\ldots)=0.
$$

v7.2 adds a concrete precedent from theoretical physics in which Archimedean and non-Archimedean sectors are related through **physical scattering amplitudes** rather than only through an abstract number-theoretic identity.

Freund and Witten showed in 1987 that the Veneziano and Virasoro–Shapiro four-point string amplitudes can be expressed through an infinite product of non-Archimedean string amplitudes, with the resulting factorization tied to the functional equation of the Riemann zeta function. [FW87]

This establishes the following narrow proposition:

$$
\boxed{
\text{a local-global adelic relation can occur inside a genuine theoretical-physics model}
}
$$

It does **not** establish:

$$
\boxed{
\text{the physical universe is adelic}
}
$$

or

$$
\boxed{
\text{the p-adic and Archimedean sectors physically interact}
}
$$

or

$$
\boxed{
\text{the product formula is an empirical law of nature}.
}
$$

The result is therefore classified as:

$$
\boxed{\text{EXISTING THEORETICAL-PHYSICS PRECEDENT / ADAPTABLE BENCHMARK}}
$$

not as a solution to any open v7.2 physical gate.

## 5A.2 What is actually combined

The p-adic string literature keeps the physical target-space coordinates and momenta Archimedean while making the world-sheet integration variables non-Archimedean. The quantum amplitudes remain complex-valued. [FO87]

Thus the basic architecture is not

$$
\mathbb Q_p\rightarrow\mathbb R,
$$

but rather:

$$
\boxed{
\text{Archimedean target-space data}
+
\text{non-Archimedean local world-sheet structures}
}
$$

participating in related amplitude constructions.

This is especially relevant to the v7.0 decision to stop requiring that the arithmetic sector generate the continuum.

## 5A.3 Four-point benchmark

The four-point adelic relation is conventionally represented schematically as

$$
A_\infty^{(4)}
\prod_p A_p^{(4)}
=
1,
$$

with the precise normalization and the mathematical treatment of the product understood as part of the construction. [FW87]

The point for v7.2 is not the numeral $1$.

The reusable structure is:

$$
\boxed{
\text{one Archimedean amplitude}
\quad\text{paired with}\quad
\text{an Eulerian family of non-Archimedean amplitudes}
}
$$

through a global relation.

That suggests an amplitude-level realization of the abstract compatibility bridge:

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal K_N
=
{\rm Reg}\!\left[
A_\infty^{(N)}
\prod_p A_p^{(N)}
\right].
}
$$

The operator ${\rm Reg}$ is not optional notation. It records the convergence/regularization/analytic-continuation problem that must be specified before the expression can be treated as mathematically meaningful.

## 5A.4 Regularization is a first-class debt

Later literature showed that the raw infinite products require care. Aref'eva, Dragović and Volovich emphasized that the direct product of the p-adic amplitudes considered by Freund and Witten is divergent in the relevant setting, while a product involving moduli has better convergence properties. [ADV88]

Jepsen's later analysis makes the same methodological issue central: the four-point adelic amplitude requires a prescription for the product, and naive factorization of infinite products is not automatically legitimate. [JP23]

Therefore v7.2 imposes:

\[
\boxed{
\text{No adelic amplitude claim without an explicit convergence domain and product prescription.}
}
\]

Every candidate $\mathcal K_N$ must record:

- convergence domain;
- regularization or analytic-continuation prescription;
- dependence on normalization/couplings;
- invariance under admissible regrouping or representation changes;
- whether the resulting object has a physical kinematic domain.

A regularized identity is not automatically invalid. It simply carries **regularization debt** until the prescription is justified.

## 5A.5 Higher-point amplitudes are an adversarial test

The four-point formula must not be silently generalized to all scattering processes.

The 1988/1989 literature and later work studied higher-point adelic amplitudes. Five-point products can be treated in suitable kinematic regimes, but the global relation is not simply the same constant as at four points. The later analysis finds explicit nonconstant expressions in special regimes and shows that the five-point adelic amplitude need not be represented by one globally analytic function. [BOFW88, BFM88, JP23]

This has two consequences.

First:

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal K_N\neq1
}
$$

is not a failure by itself. The physically relevant question is whether a mathematically well-defined, symmetry-compatible and null-separating $\mathcal K_N$ exists.

Second:

$$
\boxed{
\text{four-point simplicity is not evidence of universal simplicity}.
}
$$

The more complicated higher-point behavior is therefore retained as an adversarial benchmark.

The literature also shows that the kinematic regions in which certain higher-point products converge need not coincide with the physical scattering region. v7.2 therefore forbids treating those mathematical regions as empirical predictions without a separate physical-domain argument. [JP23]

## 5A.6 What Freund–Witten contributes to the five bridge classes

The benchmark maps primarily to:

The strongest direct mapping is

$$

+
\text{p-adic amplitudes}
\rightarrow
\text{global relation}.
$$

### B2 — Constraint

The global relation can be read as an admissibility condition on a family of local amplitudes.
