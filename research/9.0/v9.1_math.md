# 1. Arithmetic firewall

## Theorem 1 — finite single-cycle conjugacy

Let \(X\) be a finite set with \(|X|=N\), and let \(F:X\to X\) be a single-cycle permutation.

Then \(F\) is conjugate to
\[
\sigma(k)=k+1\pmod N.
\]

### Proof

Choose \(x_0\in X\). Define
\[
p:\mathbb Z_N\to X,
\qquad
p(k)=F^k(x_0).
\]

Because \(F\) is a single cycle, \(p\) is bijective. Hence
\[
p\sigma(k)
=p(k+1)
=F^{k+1}(x_0)
=F(p(k)).
\]
Therefore
\[
F=p\sigma p^{-1},
\qquad
p^{-1}Fp=\sigma.
\]
\(\square\)

## Corollary 1 — conjugacy-invariant null

For any observable \(I\) satisfying
\[
I(p^{-1}Fp)=I(F),
\]
we have
\[
I(F)=I(\sigma).
\]

Thus any construction using only single-cycle conjugacy data cannot distinguish the arithmetic generator from an arithmetic-stripped cyclic clock.

**Physical status:** exact scoped mathematical result; physical no-go remains scoped to the tested arithmetic generator class.
