# Reality-First Physics v9.0
## Dismantling the Assumptions: BM + AS + CDT + CST

**Status:** candidate research architecture, not a completed theory of nature

**Governing rule**

> **Do not inherit a theory. Dismantle it. Keep only the structures that survive contact with reality, mathematics, null models, and cross-framework tests.**

---

# 0. Executive conclusion

Version 8.1 correctly identified a potentially valuable relationship between Causal Dynamical Triangulations (CDT) and Asymptotic Safety (AS): CDT provides an independent, nonperturbative construction whose phase structure, effective geometry, and scale-dependent observables can be compared with the continuum Renormalization Group (RG) program. The 2024 CDT–FRG study makes this comparison concrete, but explicitly treats the UV lattice fixed point as **putative rather than proven** [1].

Version 8.1 was nevertheless still too close to the theories it was trying to synthesize. It assigned:

\[
\text{BM}=\text{Dancer},\qquad
\text{AS}=\text{Dance},\qquad
\text{CDT}=\text{Stage}
\]

before sufficiently dismantling the assumptions inside BM, AS, and CDT.

Version 9.0 changes the rule.

We do **not** adopt BM, AS, CDT, or Causal Set Theory (CST) as intact theories. We treat each as a reservoir of candidate structures.

The resulting synthesis is therefore not:

\[
BM+AS+CDT+CST.
\]

It is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Reality}
\;\longrightarrow\;
\text{admissible physical structures}
\;\longleftarrow\;
\{BM,AS,CDT,CST\}
}
\]

The theories are applicants. Reality is the judge.

The current candidate architecture is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Causal structure}
\;\rightarrow\;
\text{geometric quantum ensemble}
\;\leftrightarrow\;
\text{RG flow}
\;\rightarrow\;
\text{effective Lorentzian spacetime}
}
\]

with a separately tested matter ontology:

\[
\boxed{
\text{effective quantum state}
\;\rightarrow\;
\text{actual configuration}
\;\rightarrow\;
\text{guidance}
}
\]

and the Born-equilibrium question treated as an unresolved measure/dynamics problem rather than an inherited BM axiom.

The critical methodological change is this:

> **CST is neither promoted to fundamental ontology nor rejected by Theorem S. CDT is neither declared the ontological Stage nor reduced to a harmless regulator. BM is neither assumed to own the fundamental Dancer nor declared irrelevant to quantum ontology. AS is neither assumed to be the true UV completion nor treated as merely a formal calculator.**

Each component must earn its physical role.

# 1. What v8.1 got right

The core insight of v8.1 remains valid:

\[
\boxed{
\text{CDT and AS are addressing related but non-identical questions.}
}
\]

CDT constructs a nonperturbative ensemble of causal triangulated geometries and investigates its phases and observables. AS describes scale dependence through a continuum theory-space flow. A useful interface is therefore not an identification of the two, but a test of whether their continuum critical structures belong to the same universality class. The 2024 CDT–FRG work explicitly compares effective actions and investigates a putative UV lattice fixed point; it does not prove the fixed point. [1]

V8.1 also correctly identified several methodological safeguards:

1. spectral dimension must not be literally identified with RG scale;
2. a continuum limit requires critical behavior rather than merely writing \(a\to0\);
3. no new particle sector should be introduced without evidence;
4. the BM equilibrium/Born problem remains open;
5. no candidate should be called a breakthrough before a quantitative discriminator exists.

Those principles survive.

The earlier document itself warned that its CDT–AS connection should be treated as a candidate bridge rather than a definitive theorem, and that the BM interface was unconstructed.

The v9.0 change is to apply that skepticism **symmetrically to all four frameworks**.

# 2. Reality-First is not a theory selector

A central methodological error in previous drafts was treating “Reality-First compliance” as a score that could choose a theory.

That is prohibited by the method itself.

A compliance test can determine:

- whether an assumption is compatible with known evidence;
- whether a mathematical construction actually performs the claimed physical job;
- whether a result survives the strongest null;
- whether a theoretical debt is exposed or hidden.

It cannot establish:

\[
\text{Theory A is true}.
\]

Therefore v9.0 abolishes language such as:

- “winner”;
- “ultimate theory”;
- “highest compliance”;
- “only survivor”;

unless referring to a **specific already-executed gate** and its explicitly scoped result.

The existing research ledger already enforces this discipline: the Spectator Obstruction is established only for a scoped frozen arithmetic/odometer class, and the claimed global structural no-go is explicitly unresolved/rejected. The ledger records the seven-wall result as established only in a frozen/1-Lipschitz p-adic class, and Theorem S as a scoped conjugacy result.

# 3. The most important correction: Theorem S has a jurisdiction

Theorem S says that the frozen finite arithmetic map is permutation-conjugate to a generic cyclic shift, so every construction that sees only the conjugacy-class data cannot distinguish that arithmetic dynamics from an arithmetic-free clock.

That is powerful.

The associated localized topological/spectral results show that the natural inverse-limit topology is zero-dimensional and that the tested dynamics-only sector cannot produce the desired archimedean quantum configuration space. The evidence ledger classifies these results as established but explicitly localized to the tested substrate/class.

The correct inference is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{The frozen arithmetic substrate cannot generate the required continuum in the tested class.}
}
\]

The incorrect inference is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{No discrete causal substrate can underlie an effective continuum.}
}
\]

The latter does not follow.

Causal-set theory is not the frozen p-adic odometer system. It has a different primitive structure:

\[
C=(E,\prec),
\]

where \(E\) is a locally finite set and \(\prec\) is a partial order.

CDT is also not the frozen p-adic system. Its discrete configurations are causal triangulated geometries with geometric adjacency and metric edge data.

Therefore:

\[
\text{Theorem S}\not\Rightarrow\text{CST killed},
\]

and

\[
\text{Theorem S}\not\Rightarrow\text{CDT validated}.
\]

It is a firewall against a **specific failed arithmetic-generation route**.

This distinction is now constitutional.

# 4. A second correction: “calculus generates, algebra cannot” is a heuristic, not a universal theorem

The Reality-First maps correctly observe that calculus is the native language of continuous dynamics while algebra is often excellent at states, symmetries, representations and invariants. But that observation must not be turned into an ontological theorem.

Discrete mathematics can approximate differential equations.

Graph Laplacians can converge to continuum Laplacians under suitable scaling.

Discrete actions can converge to continuum actions.

Lattice field theories are explicitly constructed to recover continuum observables in appropriate limits.

Conversely, differential equations can be mathematically written on an arbitrarily stipulated continuum without explaining why that continuum is physically present.

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\text{mathematical language does not determine ontological priority by itself.}
}
\]

The question remains:

> What physical structure does the mathematics actually explain, and what has been inserted?

This is the correct extension of the Reality-First principle.

# 5. The new rule for all four theories

For each theory \(T\), decompose it into:

\[
T=
\underbrace{A_T}_{\text{assumptions}}
+
\underbrace{M_T}_{\text{mathematics}}
+
\underbrace{R_T}_{\text{physical results}}
+
\underbrace{D_T}_{\text{debts}}.
\]

Then ask four separate questions.

### Q1 — Which assumption is actually needed?

Do not inherit it merely because the original theory does.

### Q2 — Does the mathematics perform physical work?

A beautiful representation, invariant, or limit is not enough.

### Q3 — What survives a null?

Replace the physical structure with the strongest simpler alternative and see what remains.

### Q4 — What must be imported?

Every continuum, time parameter, measure, state space, coupling, topology, or dimension inserted by hand becomes a ledger item.

This decomposition is now the common audit protocol for BM, AS, CDT, and CST.

# 6. DANCER — dismantling Bohmian Mechanics

Bohmian mechanics contributes something genuinely distinctive:

\[
\boxed{
\text{actual configurations rather than only wave-function ontology}
}
\]

That is the part worth preserving.

But standard BM carries several assumptions that should **not** automatically be inherited.

## 6.1 BM assumption: a fixed configuration space

Nonrelativistic BM normally starts with:

\[
Q(t)\in\mathbb R^{3N}
\]

and a wavefunction

\[
\psi(q,t).
\]

This is phenomenologically successful, but in a quantum-gravity synthesis it is not innocuous.

It presupposes:

- spatial dimensionality;
- particle number;
- a smooth configuration manifold;
- a time parameter;
- a measure on configuration space.

Those are precisely the structures the Stage sector is supposed to explain.

Therefore v9.0 retains:

\[
\text{actual configuration}
\]

but rejects:

\[
\boxed{
\mathbb R^{3N}\text{ as an unexplained fundamental input}.
}
\]

The configuration space becomes a **derived effective object** unless a deeper construction demonstrates otherwise.

## 6.2 BM assumption: particle ontology is fundamental

This is also too restrictive for QFT.

Bohmian QFT already has formulations with variable particle number and explicit creation/annihilation events [2].

Therefore the synthesis should not freeze onto:

\[
Q=(\mathbf x_1,\ldots,\mathbf x_N).
\]

The retained principle is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{there are actual physical beables/configurations}.
}
\]

Their detailed ontology is left open until the QFT/Stage sector selects it.

## 6.3 BM assumption: a preferred foliation is mandatory

This cannot be promoted as an unconditional BM axiom.

Relativistic Bohmian formulations differ in how they handle hypersurfaces and foliation. Some require additional foliation structure; other constructions seek covariant formulations.

Therefore v9.0 does not identify CDT's foliation with BM's foliation by fiat.

The real question is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Does the Stage supply a physically acceptable structure on which BM guidance can be formulated covariantly?}
}
\]

Possible outcomes include:

1. a dynamically selected foliation;
2. a covariant law using hypersurface configurations;
3. a foliation-free formulation;
4. another ontology.

Reality chooses.

## 6.4 BM assumption: \(\rho=|\psi|^2\) is simply fundamental

Standard BM can take quantum equilibrium as a primitive statistical hypothesis.

That is acceptable as an interpretation of ordinary quantum mechanics but not as a completed explanation inside a TOE.

V9.0 therefore retains:

\[
\text{equivariance}
\]

as a structural property to preserve, but treats the origin of the equilibrium measure as an open interface:

\[
\boxed{
\mu_{\rm Stage}\stackrel{?}{\longrightarrow}|\psi|^2.
}
\]

Valentini-type relaxation remains a candidate mechanism, not the theorem that creates the Born rule.

## 6.5 BM assumption: the wavefunction dynamics is already given

In ordinary BM this is fine.

In the synthesis it becomes conditional.

We need:

\[
\Psi_{\rm Stage}
\]

or an effective state equation supplied by the quantum-spacetime sector.

Thus the BM contribution is narrowed to:

\[
\boxed{
\text{beables + guidance + equivariant dynamics}
}
\]

not a complete theory of the universe.

# 7. DANCE — dismantling Asymptotic Safety

AS contributes one of the strongest mathematical ideas in the synthesis:

\[
\boxed{
\text{physical laws may organize into a finite-dimensional UV critical surface.}
}
\]

That is worth testing.

But AS has assumptions of its own.

## 7.1 AS assumption: the continuum metric field is already the right microscopic variable

FRG calculations begin with an effective action such as

\[
\Gamma_k[g,\ldots].
\]

This is not wrong. It is precisely the language of a continuum effective description.

But it does not automatically establish that \(g_{\mu\nu}\) is the fundamental microscopic ontology.

Therefore the synthesis uses:

\[
\boxed{
\Gamma_k=\text{candidate effective description}
}
\]

rather than

\[
\Gamma_k=\text{fundamental microscopic law}.
\]

This permits a microscopic description other than a continuum metric while retaining the FRG as an effective-scale language.

## 7.2 AS assumption: a non-Gaussian fixed point exists

This remains a hypothesis supported by a large body of calculations, not a theorem of the full theory.

Reviews describe substantial evidence and compelling hints based mainly on functional-RG calculations [3].

Therefore:

\[
g_i(k)\rightarrow g_i^\ast
\]

is retained as a **candidate structure**.

It cannot be promoted to fact until robustness is established against:

- truncation enlargement;
- regulator choice;
- gauge fixing;
- parametrization;
- field redefinitions;
- matter content;
- independent nonperturbative formulations.

## 7.3 AS assumption: \(k\) is a physical scale

This is another hidden assumption.

The RG scale \(k\) is a coarse-graining parameter. It is not automatically:

- proper time;
- physical energy;
- diffusion time;
- causal-set cardinality;
- cosmological time.

So we explicitly prohibit:

\[
k=\sigma^{-1/2}
\]

unless a derivation establishes the map.

The correct question is whether there exists some physically justified correspondence:

\[
\boxed{
k=f(\sigma,\text{observable class},\text{phase}).
}
\]

## 7.4 AS assumption: fixed-point relevance alone gives physical prediction

It does not.

A fixed point can exist mathematically and still fail to describe our universe.

Therefore AS must pass:

\[
\text{existence}
\rightarrow
\text{universality}
\rightarrow
\text{IR connection}
\rightarrow
\text{phenomenology}.
\]

The synthesis inherits none of these automatically.

# 8. STAGE — dismantling CDT

CDT remains extremely valuable, but v9.0 removes the old assumption that “CDT is the Stage.”

Current CDT work describes the framework as a lattice formulation whose regularized path integral sums over triangulated spacetimes and aims at a nonperturbative continuum theory through an appropriate scaling limit. Recent reviews and 2026 work also describe an emergent de Sitter-like macroscopic phase and short-distance spectral dimension near two, while the UV fixed-point/continuum program remains active [4][5].

## 8.1 CDT assumption: four-dimensional spacetime topology

Standard four-dimensional CDT chooses a particular class of foliated triangulations and a fixed global topology.

That is an extraordinarily useful controlled setup.

But it means:

\[
d=4
\]

and substantial parts of the topology are **inputs to the model**, not universally derived outputs.

Therefore the synthesis retains:

\[
\boxed{
\text{controlled causal geometric ensemble}
}
\]

but does not claim:

\[
\boxed{
\text{CDT derives the universe's dimension and topology from nothing}.
}
\]

## 8.2 CDT assumption: global foliation

The standard formulation uses a preferred slicing.

This may be physically harmless, emergent, gauge-like, or an artifact of the chosen formulation.

We do not decide in advance.

Thus:

\[
\text{CDT foliation}
\]

becomes a variable to test rather than a constitutional fact.

## 8.3 CDT assumption: discrete simplices are the correct microscopic degrees of freedom

Not inherited.

The physical content may ultimately lie in:

- causal relations;
- universality class;
- effective geometry;
- correlation functions;
- spectral observables;
- or some more abstract equivalence class.

The simplex is a computational representation until shown otherwise.

## 8.4 CDT assumption: Wick rotation defines the physical theory

No.

A well-defined Wick rotation is important because it makes numerical evaluation possible, but the physical Lorentzian meaning and Euclidean computational representation must remain distinct.

Current CDT descriptions explicitly distinguish Lorentzian building blocks from the Wick-rotated statistical representation used for simulations [4].

So the retained structure is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Lorentzian causal construction + controlled Euclidean computation}.
}
\]

## 8.5 CDT assumption: spectral dimension is physical dimension

Rejected.

CDT's \(D_S(\sigma)\) is a diffusion-derived observable.

It is not automatically:

- topological dimension;
- Hausdorff dimension;
- configuration-space dimension;
- number of particle degrees of freedom.

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{
D_S=\text{Stage fingerprint}
}
\]

not

\[
D_S=\text{ontological dimension}.
\]

# 9. STAGE — dismantling Causal Set Theory

CST supplies a different set of potentially valuable structures:

\[
\boxed{
\text{causal order + local finiteness + cardinality}.
}
\]

The central CST hypothesis is that continuum spacetime is an approximation to an underlying locally finite poset. CST literature explicitly treats manifold-likeness, geometric reconstruction, dynamics, and continuum emergence as separate problems [6].

We retain these as candidates, not as dogma.

## 9.1 CST assumption: causal order is fundamental

This is attractive because causal order is unusually close to an empirically anchored structural feature of relativity.

But:

\[
\text{causal order}
\]

alone does not automatically provide the entire metric.

CST adds local finiteness/cardinality precisely to supply volume information.

The question for v9.0 is therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Can order + number dynamically reconstruct all required continuum geometry?}
}
\]

## 9.2 CST assumption: spacetime is fundamentally discrete

Not adopted.

This is a hypothesis, not an established fact.

It remains on the candidate list because CST offers a particularly economical encoding of causal structure, not because discreteness has been proven.

## 9.3 CST assumption: continuum emergence is generic

Definitely not adopted.

Most causal sets are not manifoldlike, and the entropy problem is a recognized central issue [6][7].

Therefore a successful synthesis requires:

\[
\boxed{
\text{dynamics must select a stable manifoldlike sector.}
}
\]

This is one of the strongest CST obligations to the whole program.

## 9.4 CST assumption: Poisson sprinkling is ontological evidence

Rejected.

Poisson sprinkling starts with a continuum geometry and generates a causet approximation from it.

Therefore it establishes:

\[
(M,g)\rightarrow C
\]

but does not by itself establish:

\[
C\rightarrow(M,g).
\]

This becomes a calibration/reconstruction tool, not a proof of fundamental causet ontology.

## 9.5 CST assumption: classical sequential growth is the quantum Dance

Rejected.

Classical sequential growth is a stochastic dynamics. It provides an important template, but CST's quantum dynamics remains an active research problem. The CST literature explicitly distinguishes classical sequential-growth models from the still-needed viable quantum dynamics [6].

So:

\[
\boxed{
CSG=\text{candidate causal growth mechanism}
}
\]

not

\[
CSG=\text{quantum gravity}.
\]

# 17. The revised mathematical philosophy

Version 9.0 adopts a stricter rule:

> **Mathematics is admitted by physical function, not by theoretical reputation.**

The program therefore assigns no permanent ownership to mathematics.

## Differential geometry

Admitted when it calculates:

- causal cones;
- curvature;
- volume;
- geodesics;
- continuum limits.

## Functional analysis

Admitted when it describes:

- states;
- operators;
- spectra;
- self-adjoint dynamics.

## Algebra and representation theory

Admitted when:

- symmetry is physically operative;
- representation labels correspond to observables;
- selection rules have measurable consequences.

## Combinatorics

Admitted when:

- causal/order structure is physically meaningful;
- finite-size scaling is computed;
- discrete histories are the actual computational object.

## RG and critical phenomena

Admitted when:

- coarse-graining changes measurable physics;
- fixed points are independently testable;
- universality connects microscopics to observables.

## Arithmetic

No automatic admission.

The prior arithmetic program's strongest negative result remains intact: the frozen p-adic/odometer construction does not own continuum dynamics in the tested class.

Any arithmetic component of the final theory therefore requires a new ownership result.
