## 1. The Adelic Constraint-Coupling Architecture (ACCA)

### 1.1 Foundational postulates

Each postulate is forced by a specific wall (W), reality constraint (R), or corner (C) identified in the G3.5 audit.

**Postulate P0 — Ambient continuum (forced by W: trivial-flow, confinement; by R: all continuum observations).**
The quantum continuum sector — configuration space, Hilbert structure, Schrödinger-class dynamics — is ambient and axiomatic. It is not derived from arithmetic. This is not a concession; it is the experimental fact that continuum QM is correct in its tested regime, joined to the theorem that no admissible arithmetic dynamics of the audited classes (C1–C3) generates it natively.

**Postulate P1 — Adelic co-equality (forced by number-theoretic structure; by W: topological obstruction).**
The $p$-adic and real completions of $\mathbb{Q}$ are siblings, not parent and child. The arithmetic sector and the continuum sector are co-equal partners in a global adelic structure. They are linked by a global constraint (analogous to the Hasse principle), not by one emerging from the other.

**Postulate P2 — Admissibility constraint (forced by W: Theorem S null-equivalence; by R: Bell/measurement-dependence; by C: exact quantization corners).**
Physical states form a strict subset of the mathematically writable quantum states, selected by an arithmetic admissibility condition. The admissible set is characterized by prime-tower / rational-angle structure (nested roots of unity, $\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}$-valued phase data), not by generic finite-bit describability (which is null-equivalent by Phase 6).

**Postulate P3 — Physical coupling as bridge (forced by W: Spectator Obstruction; by C: arithmetic corners).**
The bridge between the arithmetic and continuum sectors is a physical interaction $\hat{H}_{\text{int}}$, not a mathematical limit. The discrete arithmetic register modulates the continuous field. The continuum is the arena in which the arithmetic rhythm is played out.

**Postulate P4 — Protected phase register (forced by C: exact quantization corners; by W: window theorems).**
Quantum theory's phase sector carries a protected arithmetic register: exact torsion phases, immune to back-action up to a derived depth, with a quantified protection/back-action trade-off. This is the program's proven structure (G3/T4). Its physical role is the machinery behind exact quantization corners.

**Postulate P5 — Pairing observables (forced by C: corners are $\mathbb{Z}$-owned; the only known torsion$\leftrightarrow\mathbb{Z}$ channel is a pairing).**
The physical signature of the arithmetic layer is pairing-type observables: physical $\mathbb{Z}$-data (charges, windings, channel numbers, fusion numbers) crossed with register-torsion yields $\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}$-valued phases — observables whose exact values are rational-angle-structured and not reproducible by a generic clock or by bare continuum QM.

**Postulate P6 — Walls as selection rules (forced by W: all seven rounds).**
The no-go theorems are not failures; they are the theory's predictions of where arithmetic does not act. Arithmetic cannot create expansion, cannot generate dispersion, cannot own the Born density at equilibrium, cannot produce native continuous time. Every future corner-catalog entry must be checked against these walls.

### 1.2 Kinematic structure

The total kinematic space is the tensor product of completions:

$$
\mathcal{H}_{\text{kin}} = \mathcal{H}_\infty \otimes \bigotimes_p \mathcal{H}_p
$$

where $\mathcal{H}_\infty = L^2(Q, dq)$ is the continuum Hilbert space (the ambient arena providing geometry, dispersion, and the modulus sector) and $\mathcal{H}_p = L^2(\mathbb{Z}_p, \mu_p)$ are the arithmetic Hilbert spaces (the clock/register sectors providing $p$-adic odometers, characters, and torsion phase). For a full adelic theory, this extends to a restricted tensor product over all places $v$, mirroring the construction of the adeles $\mathbb{A}_\mathbb{Q}$.

The physical Hilbert space is the subspace satisfying the global adelic constraint:

$$
\mathcal{H}_{\text{phys}} = \left\{ |\Psi\rangle \in \mathcal{H}_{\text{kin}} \;\middle|\; \hat{C}|\Psi\rangle = |\Psi\rangle \right\}
$$

where $\hat{C}$ enforces the matching of continuum topological invariants to arithmetic torsion invariants (the Hasse-principle analogue).

### 1.3 Dynamical structure

The total dynamics is generated by:

$$
\hat{H}_{\text{tot}} = \hat{H}_\infty \otimes \mathbb{I}_{\text{arith}} + \mathbb{I}_\infty \otimes \hat{H}_{\text{arith}} + \hat{H}_{\text{int}}
$$

where $\hat{H}_{\text{int}}$ is the physical coupling (the bridge), entangling continuum observables with arithmetic observables:

$$
\hat{H}_{\text{int}} = \sum_k \hat{O}^{(\infty)}_k \otimes \hat{O}^{(p)}_k
$$

The amplitude $\psi = \sqrt{\rho}\,e^{iS/\hbar}$ is a hybrid object: the modulus $\sqrt{\rho}$ is governed by the continuum sector $\hat{H}_\infty$, the phase $e^{iS/\hbar}$ is governed by the arithmetic register, and closure under a common effective generator requires that $\hat{H}_{\text{int}}$ couples them consistently.

## 3. Gate sequence

The gate sequence is continuous with v6.3.1 but revised to remove the four hidden biases identified in the G3.5 audit:

1. **Bottom-up spatial reductionism bias**: Gates no longer assume the arithmetic substrate must topologically become $\mathbb{R}^{3N}$.
2. **Bohmian ontological anchor bias**: G8 is demoted from a fundamental microscopic gate to an emergent IR target.
3. **Wilsonian RG bias**: The coarse-graining/RG gate is reformulated as algebraic/entanglement-based.
4. **Perturbative residual bias**: Empirical discriminators are reformulated as topological/algebraic selection rules.

### G0 — Adelic kinematic consistency

**Target:** Establish that the total kinematic space $\mathcal{H}_{\text{kin}} = \mathcal{H}_\infty \otimes \bigotimes_p \mathcal{H}_p$ is well-defined, with the continuum and arithmetic sectors as co-equal partners.

**Revised from v6.3.1 G0:** No longer requires the arithmetic substrate to generate or approximate $\mathbb{R}^{3N}$. Requires instead that the tensor-product structure is mathematically consistent and that the adelic constraint $\hat{C}$ is well-defined.

**Debt from v6.3.1:** The specific form of $\hat{C}$ remains to be constructed. The restricted tensor product over all places requires a precise definition of the "finite" condition at each place.

### G1 — Explicit microscopic map and register structure

**Target:** Define the arithmetic sector explicitly: the odometer map $F$, the character tower, the protected phase register, and the torsion structure.

**Unchanged from v6.3.1 G1:** The frozen A2 substrate $F(x) = 2x^2+3x+1$ on $\mathbb{Z}_2$ remains the reference. The pre-registered candidate budget (C1–C3) is exhausted. No new candidate is authorized until R0 identifies a high-value target and the surviving mechanism classes (M2, M4, M7) have been instantiated or definitively killed on A2.

**New requirement:** The register must be characterized by its prime-tower / rational-angle structure, not merely by its character group. The distinction between the dyadic character tower $\mu_{2^\infty}$ and the full $U(1)$ must be maintained and its physical consequences tracked.

### G2 — Joint arithmetic compatibility

**Target:** Establish that the arithmetic invariants (canonical heights, characters, valuation structure) are compatible with the adelic constraint $\hat{C}$ and the physical coupling $\hat{H}_{\text{int}}$.

**Revised from v6.3.1 G2:** The canonical-height/Markov joint-satisfiability test is retained but re-scoped. The height is no longer expected to generate the continuum; it is expected to provide arithmetic-specific selection rules that constrain the continuum dynamics via $\hat{C}$.

### G3 — Amplitude-factor existence (hybrid closure)

**Target:** Construct the complex amplitude $\psi = \sqrt{\rho}\,e^{iS/\hbar}$ as a hybrid object: the modulus $\sqrt{\rho}$ from the continuum sector, the phase $e^{iS/\hbar}$ from the arithmetic register, closed under a common effective generator via $\hat{H}_{\text{int}}$.

**Revised from v6.3.1 G3:** This is no longer a pure tame/Kronecker-factor problem on the Koopman operator. The amplitude is explicitly hybrid. The gate requires:

1. The continuum sector supplies a nontrivial modulus $\rho(q,t)$ with finite Fisher information.
2. The arithmetic sector supplies a coherent phase $S(q,t)/\hbar$ from the protected register.
3. The physical coupling $\hat{H}_{\text{int}}$ closes both under a single effective generator.
4. The coupling-ownership test (see §4) is passed: removing the arithmetic sector destroys the phase structure; removing the continuum sector destroys the modulus; the common generator depends materially on both.

**Debt:** The explicit construction of $\hat{H}_{\text{int}}$ that achieves closure remains the central open problem. This is the M7 mechanism class from Phase 5.

### G4 — Measure and algebraic RG

**Target:** Define the invariant/equilibrium measure on the physical state space $\mathcal{H}_{\text{phys}}$ and establish an algebraic or entanglement-based renormalization group flow.

**Revised from v6.3.1 G4:** The RG flow is no longer assumed to be Wilsonian momentum-scale. It may be:
- An algebraic RG on the operator algebra of the arithmetic sector.
- An entanglement-based RG (tensor network coarse-graining, MERA-type).
- A Wetterich-type functional flow on the effective average action $\Gamma_k$ of the coupled system.

The AS fixed-point structure must be compatible with the adelic constraint: the UV critical surface of the AS flow should coincide with or be restricted by the arithmetic admissibility condition.

### G5 — Projection and typed bridge

**Target:** Define the projection from the total kinematic space to the observable sector. The bridge must be a typed mathematical operation from the Phase 3 taxonomy (BR-07, BR-09, BR-11, BR-12, BR-15), with provenance audited.

**Revised from v6.3.1 G5:** The projection is no longer assumed to be a positive conditional expectation (which yields dissipative dynamics by Lemma K2'). The admissible bridge classes are:
- BR-12 (Extension/Cocycle) for the hybrid architecture.
- BR-15 (Operator-Algebra Reconstruction) for the spectral-triple route.
- BR-11 (Correlator Reconstruction) for the constructive-QFT route.

Each must pass the anti-circularity audit: the target ontology (continuum $Q$, Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}$, generator $H$) must not be imported as a primitive of the bridge.

### G6 — Born rule + no-signaling

**Target:** Recover the Born rule $\rho = |\psi|^2$ as the equilibrium distribution on the physical state space, and verify no-signaling at the setting-indexed level.

**Unchanged in substance from v6.3.1 G6:** The Born rule must be derived, not assumed. The projection scale must be explicit. No-signaling must be verified for each measurement setting, not as a single fixed correction.

**New requirement:** The Born rule must be compatible with the adelic constraint. The constraint $\hat{C}$ must not introduce observable deviations from Born quadraticity in the bulk (the GR weak-field limit requirement). Deviations are permitted only at the arithmetic corners, where they must match the R1-B discriminator bounds.

### G7 — Dynamics / generator

**Target:** Derive the effective generator $H$ governing the amplitude $\psi$. The generator must be self-adjoint and unbounded. To claim Schrödinger-class recovery, it must be a second-order local operator with quadratic dispersion.

**Revised from v6.3.1 G7:** The generator is no longer expected to emerge solely from the arithmetic Koopman operator. It is the effective generator of the coupled system:

$$
H_{\text{eff}} = H_\infty + H_{\text{arith,eff}} + H_{\text{int,eff}}
$$

The continuum sector $H_\infty$ supplies the second-order spatial structure. The arithmetic sector contributes selection rules and phase constraints via $H_{\text{int,eff}}$. The gate requires that the arithmetic contribution is not a spectator: removing it must materially change the effective generator (coupling-ownership test).

### G8 — Emergent guidance and quantum potential

**Target:** Recover Bohmian guidance $v = \nabla S / m$ and the quantum potential $Q = -\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{\nabla^2\sqrt{\rho}}{\sqrt{\rho}}$ as emergent IR effective descriptions.

**Revised from v6.3.1 G8:** This is demoted from a fundamental microscopic gate to an emergent IR target. BM is not assumed at the microscopic level. It is recovered in the semi-classical/decohered limit where the amplitude $\psi = \sqrt{\rho}\,e^{iS/\hbar}$ admits a hydrodynamic Madelung decomposition. The gate requires:

1. The Madelung equations are satisfied by the effective amplitude.
2. The quantum potential is finite and well-defined (requiring sufficient regularity of $\rho$).
3. The Wallstrom condition (phase single-valuedness, circulation quantization) is satisfied, with the arithmetic register providing the topological quantization mechanism.

### G9 — Composition / Bell / arithmetic corners

**Target:** Establish that the theory supports composition of subsystems, reproduces Bell correlations, and predicts arithmetic-specific structure at the quantization corners.

**Revised from v6.3.1 G9:** In addition to the standard composition and Bell requirements, this gate now explicitly includes:

1. **Pairing observables (P5):** The theory must predict at least one observable whose exact value is a $\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}$-valued phase produced by pairing arithmetic torsion with a $\mathbb{Z}$-valued topological datum. This is the primary arithmetic-ownership test.
2. **Corner census:** The theory must predict which quantization corners (flux, Chern, anyon, Berry) carry arithmetic-specific structure and which are null-equivalent.
3. **Bell mechanism:** The admissibility constraint must reproduce Bell violations via measurement-dependence while preserving no-signaling. The R1-A discriminator must be computed.

### G10 — QFT / Lorentz / gauge / GR downstream obligations

**Target:** Establish compatibility with quantum field theory, Lorentz symmetry, gauge invariance, and general relativity in the appropriate limits.

**Unchanged in substance from v6.3.1 G10:** These remain downstream obligations. The adelic constraint must be covariant-compatible or hidden at the $10^{-18}$+ precision level (Lemma L4 from v6.3.1).

## 4. Coupling-ownership test

This is a cross-cutting adjudication test applied whenever a candidate claims H3 (hybrid). It is an execution rule, not a new theoretical commitment. It operationalizes the existing v6.3.1 Internal-Environment/Tensor-Factorization gate (§13 of v6.3.1).

### 4.1 Procedure

For a hybrid construction claiming that arithmetic and continuum sectors are physically coupled:

1. **Sector ablation:** Remove the arithmetic sector entirely. Does the target feature (amplitude, generator, Born rule, Bell correlation) survive unchanged? If yes: SPECTATOR.
2. **Coupling ablation:** Set $\hat{H}_{\text{int}} = 0$. Do the sectors decouple and does the target feature collapse? If the target survives without coupling: the coupling is decorative, not physical.
3. **Matched arithmetic-null replacement:** Replace the arithmetic sector with the strongest admissible null (generic cyclic clock, generic finite-precision rule). Does the target feature change materially? If no: SPECTATOR.
4. **Arithmetic-independent coupling replacement:** Replace $\hat{H}_{\text{int}}$ with a generic coupling that does not reference arithmetic structure. Does the target feature survive? If yes: the arithmetic specificity of the coupling is not causally necessary.

### 4.2 Promotion criterion

HYBRID promotion requires:
- Both sectors to be independently well-defined.
- The target feature to depend materially on their coupling.
- The result not to collapse to an arithmetic-constrained or spectator construction under the strongest admissible null.

## 6. Imports from leading TOE programs

These are not adoptions of external theories. They are strategic imports of specific mathematical mechanisms that address structural gaps in the ACCA.

### 6.1 From the Amplitudes Program: positive geometry and singularity structure

**The import:** Scattering amplitudes are determined by the singularity structure and positive geometry of the amplitude space, not by a Lagrangian or spacetime lattice.

**Application to ACCA:** The arithmetic substrate does not define a lattice of spatial points. It defines the allowed singularity structure of the S-matrix. The $p$-adic arithmetic dictates the allowed rational poles and branch cuts of scattering amplitudes. The admissibility constraint $\hat{C}$ acts as a selection rule on the amplitude space, forbidding certain poles that would correspond to inadmissible counterfactual states.

**Gate impact:** Provides a route to G7 (generator) and G10 (QFT) that bypasses the spatial emergence problem entirely.

### 6.2 From Holography / AdS-CFT: entanglement as geometry

**The import:** Spacetime geometry is a macroscopic manifestation of quantum entanglement (Ryu–Takayanagi, ER=EPR).

**Application to ACCA:** The arithmetic constraint does not restrict points in space; it restricts the entanglement spectrum between subsystems. The "invariant set" is the set of allowed entanglement structures. The continuum geometry $Q \simeq \mathbb{R}^{3N}$ emerges as the thermodynamic limit of the entanglement structure defined by the arithmetic algebra.

**Gate impact:** Resolves G0/G1 (configuration space) without requiring the arithmetic substrate to topologically become $\mathbb{R}^{3N}$. The continuum emerges from entanglement, not from point-set limits.

### 6.3 From Causal Set Theory: ultrametric causality

**The import:** Spacetime can be replaced by a discrete partial order (causal relations). The $p$-adic metric is naturally ultrametric ($d(x,z) \le \max(d(x,y), d(y,z))$), which defines a hierarchical tree structure.

**Application to ACCA:** The $p$-adic dynamics is not motion through space; it is evolution through a hierarchical causal tree. The continuum spacetime emerges as the coarse-grained envelope of this discrete causal order. The ultrametric structure provides a natural notion of "scale" that can replace the Wilsonian momentum cutoff in G4.

**Gate impact:** Provides a rigorous mathematical meaning to the "arithmetic clock" and a natural scale parameter for the algebraic RG.

### 6.4 From Noncommutative Geometry: spectral triples

**The import:** A continuous manifold is entirely encoded in the spectral properties of an algebra and a Dirac operator (Connes' reconstruction theorem).

**Application to ACCA:** The arithmetic structure defines a noncommutative algebra (e.g., a crossed product $C(\mathbb{Z}_p) \rtimes_\alpha \mathbb{Z}$). The continuum geometry is the spectral shadow of this algebra. The Dirac operator $D$ encodes the differential structure.

**Gate impact:** Provides a route to G0 (geometry) and G7 (generator) via BR-15 (operator-algebra reconstruction). The AF algebra $K$-theory obstruction (OB-3 from Phase 5) blocks the commutative route but leaves the noncommutative route open.

**Debt:** D-M2-NC remains open. The noncommutative arithmetic algebra must be explicitly constructed and its $K$-theory computed.

## 7. Hypothesis classification

The three competing hypotheses are retained from v6.3.1 but re-scoped:

### H1 — Arithmetic-native

Arithmetic directly generates the relevant physical structure (continuum, amplitude, generator).

**Status after G3.5:** DISFAVORED for the frozen C1–C3 substrate classes. The 1-Lipschitz cage, zero-entropy confinement, and Spectator Obstruction block native generation. Not ruled out as a universal arithmetic no-go, but no admissible candidate exists within the pre-registered budget.

### H2 — Arithmetic-constrained

Continuum quantum theory exists independently, but arithmetic restricts the allowed physical states, sectors, parameters, phases, or observables.

**Status after G3.5/Phase 6:** OPEN and structurally indicated. The admissibility constraint $\hat{C}$ is the mathematical realization of H2. The primary risk is that the constraint is empirically silent in the bulk (GR weak-field limit) and falsified at the corners (e.g., FQHE $1/3$ state contradicts strict dyadic restriction).

### H3 — Hybrid

Arithmetic and continuum sectors coexist and are physically coupled; neither is required to generate the other. The coupling is the physical content.

**Status after G3.5:** OPEN and structurally indicated. The ACCA is explicitly an H3 architecture. The coupling-ownership test (§4) must be passed to distinguish genuine H3 from H2 with an arithmetic constraint plus an otherwise independent continuum.

## 11. Governance

### 11.1 No-sacred-cows rule

This rule applies to v7.0 itself. No postulate, gate, or architectural commitment in this document is immune from revision if a subsequent audit demonstrates a mathematical obstruction or empirical falsification.

### 11.2 Finite search budget

The pre-registered candidate budget (C1–C3) is exhausted. No new microscopic candidate (C4, C5, ...) is authorized until:
1. R0 identifies a specific high-value discriminator target.
2. The surviving mechanism classes (M2, M4, M7) have been instantiated or definitively killed on the frozen A2 substrate.
3. The candidate-generation rule (§13 of G3.5 Master Synthesis) is satisfied.

### 11.3 External red-team

An external red-team review must be conducted before any construction is promoted beyond PARTIAL status. The red-team must include:
- At least one expert in $p$-adic dynamics/ergodic theory.
- At least one expert in operator algebras/noncommutative geometry.
- At least one expert in quantum foundations/Bell inequalities.
- At least one expert in asymptotic safety/quantum gravity.

### 11.4 Reality-First / Ultimate Acid Test

> **Reality does not owe the theory coherence. The theory owes reality correspondence.**

If a construction is mathematically beautiful but fails to reproduce or predict what the physical world demonstrably does, it is killed or downgraded to debt. No post-hoc rescue via ad hoc mechanisms, hidden variables, or new axioms is permitted.

### 11.5 Occam guillotine

If a proposed mechanism adds structure without resolving a specific gate or discriminator, it is removed. The symmetric AS redundancy kill condition is retained: if the AS sector can reproduce a target feature without the arithmetic sector, the arithmetic contribution to that feature is SPECTATOR.
