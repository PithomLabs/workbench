## 1. THE STORY IN 60 SECONDS

We started by trying to make a tiny arithmetic code *grow into* quantum mechanics. We built the smallest honest model (a frozen arithmetic clock), attacked it seven times with full discipline, and it died seven times — each death discovering a wall where arithmetic *cannot* reach the physics we wanted. Then we audited ourselves and found the real error: **we asked the wrong question.** Physics is not uniformly smooth — it has exact, protected, measurable arithmetic "corners" (quantized flux, Chern numbers, anyon phases) owned by *counting*-arithmetic, not by our clock's *cycle*-arithmetic. And mathematics already contains a 75-year-old proven structure (Tate's adelic framework) with exactly the three-layer shape our theory needs: coequal sectors, product coupling, arithmetic-as-lawbook. So the program pivoted: **stop asking if arithmetic generates physics; ask where nature already uses arithmetic, and whether our specific kind has an address there.** The tests are now running — cheapest first — and every outcome is pre-registered as publishable.

## 2. THE THEORY IN ONE BREATH

> **The quantum world is a continuum stage doing what stages do (dispersion, superposition, measurement) — over which an arithmetic lawbook operates as a constraint: deciding which states are real, protecting exact phases, forbidding configurations. The lawbook is invisible to generic clocks by theorem, silent in all tested experiments by design, and detectable only through "pairing" observables — where the clock's cycle-arithmetic meets nature's counting-arithmetic. It doesn't generate physics. It licenses what physics may do.**

Three layers, one sentence each:
- **Stage** (Bohmian + asymptotic safety): the smooth quantum world — *axiomatic, not derived*; licensed by a scale-flow whose deep structure is witnessed in calculations but not proven.
- **Lawbook** (invariant set, sharpened): an arithmetic admissibility rule deciding which quantum states are real — *our hypothesis, not yet established*.
- **Coupling**: the clock modulates the stage through a gauge-type interaction (prototype: the Aharonov–Bohm effect) — *mechanism exists in nature; our instance is unbuilt*.

## 9. THE DURABLE INSIGHTS (survive even if the theory dies)

1. **Arithmetic in nature is a lawbook, not a generator** — every exact-discrete structure in physics *constrains*; none *creates*.
2. **The spectator test is the sharpest tool in foundational research** — "could a generic clock do this?" killed seven candidates in seven rounds and costs almost nothing to run.
3. **Layered theories need their own gates** — they die from internal inconsistency and smuggling, not from missing derivations; the old derivation-gates couldn't see those failure modes.
4. **Wave/particle complementarity has a mechanism shape** — a protection/visibility trade-off with a depth parameter is a real, quantified version of what is usually narrated philosophically.
5. **Order of operations is the whole game** — specify the target, derive the impossibilities, run the nulls, *then* freeze and build. The seven-round cost was the price of running this backwards.
