## 1. Why the Three Foundational Theories Must Be Reconsidered

### 1.1 The general principle

A theory that is successful in a regime need not be fundamental in that regime's deepest possible sense. The same logic applies to BM, IST, and AS. The program therefore asks, for every foundational statement:

Is this an observed regularity, an established mathematical theorem, a model-dependent assumption, a convenient representation, or an unconstructed bridge?

Those categories must never be silently collapsed.

### 1.2 Bohmian Mechanics: demoted to emergent IR ontology

Standard BM gives a precise and valuable ontology at the macroscopic scale. But v7.0 explicitly demotes BM from a foundational pillar to a **derived IR effective description**. The reasons:

- The G3.5 audit established that the arithmetic substrate cannot generate the continuum configuration space, the Schrödinger generator, or the Born density from scratch. Therefore BM's continuum trajectories cannot be the microscopic primitive.
- BM is notoriously difficult to reconcile with QFT, fermions, and Lorentz invariance at the fundamental level. Hardcoding the first-order guidance law $v = \nabla S / m$ as a microscopic gate (v6.3.1 G8) created an artificial bottleneck.
- The correct sequence is: derive the unitary algebra and the Born statistics first; recover Bohmian trajectories as the semi-classical, decohered, low-energy limit.

**What survives from BM:** definite configurations, interference-capable dynamical state, guidance structure, coherent measurement account — all as IR targets.

**What is demoted:** continuous configuration space as primitive, exact unitary Schrödinger evolution at every scale, Born distribution as fundamental postulate, fixed particle number, inserted preferred foliation, Markovianity, textbook first-order guidance law as microscopic law, quantum potential as primitive.

### 1.3 Invariant Set Theory: reframed as constraint architecture

IST contributes the most distinctive ontology to the program. Under v7.0, its role is sharpened:

**The invariant set is not a geometric fractal imposed on a continuous state space. It is the spectral shadow of a non-commutative arithmetic algebra acting as a global admissibility constraint.**

The following cannot be treated as conclusions before the constraint is constructed:
- the existence of a physically realized invariant set $I_U$;
- a positive-codimension fractal geometry;
- a particular $p$-adic metric;
- a particular prime;
- a hand-set information capacity $L$;
- measurement-independence violation as a separate postulate.

**The retained IST commitment:** A discrete arithmetic constraint layer is the incumbent hypothesis. The constraint acts as a selection rule on the ambient continuum, not as a generator of the continuum.

**The sharpened IST commitment:** The constraint's currency is nested prime-tower torsion; its observable signatures are rational-angle structures produced by pairing its phases with the whole-number data of topology; its influence is invisible to generic clocks by theorem, present in the corner catalog by measurement, and bounded by no-go walls we have proven.

### 1.4 Asymptotic Safety: elevated to dynamical bridge

AS contributes the scale-dependent dynamics that connects the UV constraint to the IR ontology. Under v7.0, AS is elevated from a "discipline" to the **dynamical engine** of the theory:

- The invariant set $I_U$ is identified with the UV critical surface $W^s(\Gamma_*)$ of the AS flow.
- The "gaps" in the invariant set are directions in theory space that are RG-irrelevant or forbidden by the UV fixed point structure.
- The critical exponents of the fixed point dictate the scaling laws of the arithmetic constraint across energy regimes, providing parameter-free predictions for the R1 discriminators.

The Wetterich/effective-average-action framework is adopted as a tool family, not a sacred equation. If the discrete substrate is better represented by an algebraic flow, a transfer operator, or an entanglement-based RG, the program uses the structure that actually works.

### 1.5 The four dismantled biases from v6.3.1

v7.0 explicitly identifies and corrects four hidden biases that were embedded in the v6.3.1 gate structure:

**Bias 1 — Bottom-Up Spatial Reductionism.** v6.3.1 assumed the continuum must emerge as the topological limit of the discrete state space. This is structurally blocked by the 1-Lipschitz confinement theorem. v7.0 replaces spatial emergence with **algebraic/entanglement emergence**: the continuum is ambient; the arithmetic constrains its algebra of observables.

**Bias 2 — Bohmian Ontological Anchor.** v6.3.1 G8 demanded derivation of the first-order guidance law as a fundamental gate. v7.0 demotes this to an IR effective phenomenon. The fundamental gate is the derivation of the unitary algebra and the Born statistics.

**Bias 3 — Wilsonian RG Presupposition.** v6.3.1 assumed a standard Wilsonian momentum-scale flow, which presupposes a pre-existing background spacetime. v7.0 permits **algebraic RG**, **entanglement-based RG**, and **real-space RG** as equally valid coarse-graining frameworks.

**Bias 4 — Perturbative Residual Bias.** v6.3.1's empirical program looked for smooth perturbative deviations ($\Delta c/c$, Born residuals). v7.0 recognizes that an arithmetic constraint layer produces **hard selection rules, topological gaps, and non-perturbative discrete jumps**, not smooth blurs.

## 30. The Elephant and the End State

The Blind Men and the Elephant analogy is the epistemic constitution of the program. BM, IST, and AS are not entitled to be the elephant merely because each illuminates a genuine structural feature. The elephant is whatever survives translation, adversarial testing, and subtraction.

What remains if BM, IST, AS, E8, and even this research program are all allowed to be wrong?

A successful outcome should be structurally sparse: an ambient continuum, a small arithmetic constraint, a physical coupling, a scale-dependent flow, and the least additional structure required for relativistic and gravitational recovery.

The program is successful even when the answer is negative, provided the failure is localized, reproducible, and reduces the hypothesis space.

**Final doctrine:** Reality first; construct before interpretation; type before identification; pre-register before comparison; separate equilibrium destination from relaxation path; decompose phase from modulus; treat the continuum as ambient and the arithmetic as lawbook; compare null models symmetrically; and let failure remove structure rather than add it.
