# 0. Executive Thesis

The program starts from an uncomfortable premise:

> The three source theories may all contain genuine physical insight while also carrying assumptions that cannot safely be imported into a common foundational theory. Among these assumptions is the paradigm itself — the generator-first realism that v6.3.1 took for granted.

Bohmian Mechanics (BM), Invariant Set Theory (IST), and Asymptotic Safety (AS) therefore undergo the same treatment. None is sacred. Neither is the paradigm that organized them.

**The paradigm revision.** v6.3.1 assumed that foundational physics has a "bottom layer" and that the theory's job is to derive observable physics from that layer. Seven phases of honest kills showed this paradigm could not be made to work: the arithmetic substrate kept turning out to be a spectator. The adelic reformulation removes the assumption. There is no bottom layer. There are co-equal sectors connected by a flow.

The program retains only a small architectural core:

1. an **ambient adelic state space** H = L²(𝔸_ℚ/ℚ);
2. a **spectral triple** (𝒜, H, D) with the convolution algebra 𝒜 on the idele class group;
3. a **scaling flow** σ_s generated by the idelic scaling action;
4. an **arithmetic constraint** — physical states are ℚ*-invariant.

Everything else is a **derived target, theorem obligation, or empirical question**.

The intended architecture is therefore not:

$$
\text{IST} + \text{AS} + \text{BM}
$$

as three stacked theories, and not a generator pipeline from a microscopic layer. It is:

$$
\text{Adelic ambient space}

\text{co-equal sectors}
\;\xrightarrow{\mathbb{Q}^*\text{-invariance}}\;
\text{physical states}
\;\xrightarrow{\text{archimedean projection}}\;
\text{known physics}.
$$

The phrase **"one flow" is deliberately no longer used as an established architectural fact**. The correct status is **one candidate flow whose properties are themselves theorem targets**.

The central question is correspondingly sharp:

$$

\text{Does a Dirac-type operator exist on the adele class space, and does its spectrum on the } \mathbb{Q}^*\text{-invariant subspace reproduce the exact quantization data of quantum mechanics?}

The research program lives or dies on that question.

# 1. Why the Three Foundational Theories Must Be Reconsidered

## 1.1 The general principle

A theory that is successful in a regime need not be fundamental in that regime's deepest possible sense.

A low-energy theory may correctly describe experiments while encoding assumptions that become illegitimate when its domain of validity is extended beyond the scale at which those assumptions were justified.

The same logic applies to BM, IST, and AS — and to the generator-first paradigm itself.

The program therefore asks, for every foundational statement:

> Is this an observed regularity, an established mathematical theorem, a model-dependent assumption, a convenient representation, or an unconstructed bridge?

Those categories must never be silently collapsed.

## 1.2 Bohmian Mechanics: what must not simply be assumed

Standard BM gives a precise and valuable ontology: configurations are definite, and their evolution is tied to a wavefunction.

But the tripartite program cannot promote the following to microscopic axioms:

- literal continuum configuration space;
- exact unitary Schrödinger evolution as primitive;
- the Born distribution as a fundamental equilibrium postulate;
- fixed particle number as fundamental;
- an inserted preferred foliation;
- Markovian, memoryless effective dynamics;
- the first-order guidance law as the microscopic law;
- the quantum potential as primitive;
- **and, new in v7, the assumption that BM's ontology is at a different level from the continuum it describes**.

In the adelic formulation, BM is the **archimedean-sector effective description** of the constrained subspace. Its trajectories are not fundamental; they are the IR shadows of the flow σ_s on the ℚ*-invariant states.

## 1.3 Invariant Set Theory: what must not simply be assumed

IST contributes the most distinctive ontology to the program, and receives the strongest scrutiny.

The following cannot be treated as conclusions before the adelic spectral triple is constructed:

- the existence of a physically realized invariant set I_U as a fractal subset of a continuum Hilbert space;
- a positive-codimension fractal geometry;
- a particular p-adic metric as an *external* addition;
- a particular prime as a *chosen* feature;
- a hand-set information capacity L;
- a hand-set update/collapse rate;
- a rational/irrational definability cut as an *axiom*;
- measurement-independence violation as a *separate* postulate;
- a chaos/attractor structure as the *sole* source of exactness.

The research program retains one foundational commitment more openly than v6.3.1 did:

> **Physical states live on an adelic structure in which the archimedean and non-archimedean sectors are co-equal.**

This is not declared true. It is declared **the incumbent hypothesis under test**.

The arithmetic commitment is **recast**: not "arithmetic dynamics generates quantum structure," but "arithmetic invariance selects physical states." Even "arithmetic" is typed: the permitted structure is the idelic class group C_ℚ = 𝔸_ℚ*/ℚ* and its representations, not an arbitrary arithmetic dynamical system.

### 1.3.1 Why the adelic commitment remains the incumbent hypothesis

The adelic commitment is a research bet, not a protected conclusion. It is retained because the incumbent framework must simultaneously support:

- **discreteness and continuity as co-equal features**, without a hierarchy;
- **a natural invariant measure** on physical states (Haar on the constrained subspace);
- **a natural scaling flow** whose fixed points have physical meaning;
- **a natural appearance of exact quantizations** from the p-adic sector;
- **a natural reduction to continuum QM in the IR**.

The adele ring provides all five. No other known mathematical structure does. This is the reason for the bet.

The arithmetic layer is abandoned if the adelic spectral triple cannot be constructed, or if its spectrum on the ℚ*-invariant subspace does not encode the exact quantization data of quantum mechanics.

## 1.4 Asymptotic Safety: what must not simply be assumed

AS contributes the RG discipline, but the tripartite program must not turn AS vocabulary into metaphysics.

The following remain open:

- existence of the relevant fixed point for the *adelic* substrate, not merely the gravitational sector;
- physical meaning of its fixed-point coordinates in adelic terms;
- relation between the idelic scaling flow σ_s and the Wilsonian RG flow (this is a **specific theorem target**, not an assumption);
- regulator/scheme independence under the required truncations;
- compatibility with the emergent matter sector;
- Euclidean-to-Lorentzian continuation;
- whether the continuum description is itself a projection of the adelic structure.

The Wetterich/effective-average-action framework is adopted as a **tool family**, not a sacred equation. If the exact discrete coarse-graining is better represented by the idelic scaling flow (which v7 proposes as the bridge), the program must use that structure.

# 2. EBP v2.1: The Constitutional Layer

*Unchanged from v6.3.1.*

## 2.1 The governing rule

EBP v2.1 has one central asymmetry:

> **Imagination is cheap. Promotion is expensive.**

An idea may enter without proof, map, invariant, toy model, null model, or formalization. Promotion requires its obligations to be explicit and sufficiently retired.

The minimum rule:

```text
Ideas enter free.
Promotion costs debt.
Debt does not kill.
Debt is forever payable.
New evidence creates new debt.
Promotion never means truth.
```

## 2.2 Why EBP is necessary

A TOE program is unusually vulnerable to motivated reasoning. EBP prevents an attractive narrative from silently acquiring the status of a physical axiom.

## 2.3 Debt classes

Every serious bridge eventually owes some combination of:

- `needMap` — domain, codomain, translation rule;
- `needInvariant` — what survives the bridge;
- `needToyCheck` — what finite computation can fail early;
- `needNullModel` — could a cheaper theory explain the same phenomenon;
- `needObstruction` — theorem, counterexample, or incompatibility;
- `needFaithfulnessReview` — does the formal statement encode the intended physical claim.

Two additional classes remain from v6.3:

- `needInitialCondition` — preparation requirements for a relaxation argument;
- `needRegularity` — smoothness, phase, nodal, and domain assumptions.

Two additional classes are promoted in v7.0:

- `needDualityCheck` — does the claimed duality between sectors preserve physical content?
- `needConstraintOwnership` — is the constraint causally necessary, or is it reproduced by a generic null?

## 2.4 Promotion governance

**Owner:** constructs the claim and evidence.

**Independent reviewer:** checks the debt retirement and may file a one-sentence objection.

**External red-team:** runs at milestone gates.

For the current program, the independent reviewer must be a **different model family and/or a named human researcher**, not the same model instance that constructed the claim.

A promoted claim can be unpromoted by the reviewer, but only with a written reason tied to a defined debt class.

# 5. The Adelic Spectral Triple: Core Structure

This is the central structural change of v7.0.

## 5.1 The ambient state space

Let **𝔸_ℚ** denote the adele ring of ℚ:

$$
\mathbb{A}_\mathbb{Q} = \mathbb{R} \times \prod'_p \mathbb{Q}_p
$$

with the restricted product. Let **C_ℚ = 𝔸_ℚ* / ℚ*** denote the idele class group.

The **ambient state space** is:

$$
\boxed{
H := L^2(\mathbb{A}_\mathbb{Q} / \mathbb{Q}, d\mu_{\rm Haar})
}
$$

where 𝔸_ℚ/ℚ is the adele class space (additive quotient) and dμ_Haar is the canonical Haar measure.

$H$ carries a natural unitary representation of 𝔸_ℚ* by scaling. The restriction to the real scaling subgroup is the **scaling flow**:

$$
\sigma_s := U_{e^s}, \qquad s \in \mathbb{R}.
$$

This is a strongly continuous one-parameter unitary group. Its generator is the **scaling operator** D_σ = −i d/ds σ_s|_{s=0}, an unbounded self-adjoint operator on H.

## 5.2 The algebra and the Dirac operator

Let **𝒜 := C_c^∞(C_ℚ)**, the convolution algebra on the idele class group.

The **adelic spectral triple** is:

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal{S} := (\mathcal{A}, H, D)
}
$$

where $D$ is a Dirac-type operator on H satisfying the **five adelic conditions**:

1. **ℚ*-commutativity.** D commutes with the diagonal ℚ* action on H.
2. **Chirality.** D anticommutes with a chirality grading γ.
3. **Archimedean reality.** The spectrum of D on the archimedean-projected subspace is real.
4. **p-adic torsion.** The spectrum of D off the archimedean axis is confined to torsion characters of the p-adic components.
5. **Product-formula consistency.** The joint spectrum satisfies the adelic product formula $\prod_v |\lambda|_v = 1$.

**Existence of D is a research target, not an assumption.** This is gate G-D (new in v7).

## 5.3 The bridge

The bridge **Φ** is *not* a map from microscopic states to theory space. It is the **scaling flow σ_s**.

> **The bridge between the discrete and the continuous is not a projection, a coarse-graining, or a limit. It is the one-parameter unitary group generated by the idelic scaling action.**

As s varies:
- **s → −∞ (UV)**: both sectors contribute equally (adelic symmetry).
- **s → +∞ (IR)**: the p-adic sector decouples (archimedean dominance).

The fixed points of σ_s are the RG fixed points. The crossover is at scale ξ₀.

## 5.4 The arithmetic constraint

Physical states are those in the **ℚ*-invariant subspace** of H. Formally:

$$
H_{\rm phys} := H^{\mathbb{Q}^*} = \{ \psi \in H : U_q \psi = \psi \text{ for all } q \in \mathbb{Q}^* \}
$$

where U_q is the diagonal ℚ* action.

The **product formula** ∏_v |x|_v = 1 is the mathematical content of this constraint: it says ℚ* lies in the kernel of the idelic norm, so the invariant subspace is the "norm-neutral" sector.

Arithmetic does not generate the states. It **selects** them.
