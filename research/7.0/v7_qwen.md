0. Executive Thesis: The Paradigm Pivot
The program starts from a brutal lesson learned in v6.3.1:
$$
\boxed{
\text{You cannot build a continuous, dispersive quantum universe bottom-up from a rigid, disconnected arithmetic grid.}
}
$$
The v6.3.1 architecture assumed that a microscopic arithmetic map $F$ could, through coarse-graining and RG flow, *generate* the continuum configuration space, the Schrödinger generator, and the Born measure. The rigorous audit of this assumption (the Spectator Obstruction) proved that 1-Lipschitz arithmetic dynamics are equicontinuous/odometer-type, possessing pure-point spectra and zero entropy. They cannot generate continuous dispersion or connected topology without importing them from the outside.

v7.0 executes a fundamental paradigm shift, analogous to the shift from Newtonian force to Einsteinian geometry:
1. **The Continuum is Ambient (or Dual):** Spacetime and continuous quantum dynamics are not *generated* by the arithmetic substrate. They exist as an ambient arena (or as a spectral dual).
2. **Arithmetic is the Constraint (The Lawbook):** The arithmetic substrate ($p$-adic/adelic structures, Prüfer character towers) acts as a *global topological and phase constraint*. It restricts which states, topological sectors, and phase holonomies are physically admissible.
3. **The Architecture is Adelic Constrained Quantum Mechanics (ACQM):** The physical Hilbert space is not the microscopic Koopman space. It is the subspace of the ambient continuum Hilbert space that survives the arithmetic constraint (e.g., matching continuous Berry phases to arithmetic torsion phases).

The central question of v7.0 is correspondingly sharp:
$$
\boxed{
\text{Can an explicit arithmetic constraint surface or spectral duality restrict an ambient continuum theory to exactly reproduce the quantized topological corners, Bell correlations, and Born statistics of reality, without importing them by hand?}
}
$$
