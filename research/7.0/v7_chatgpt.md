# 0. Executive Thesis

The previous architecture began from a strong assumption:

> A discrete/arithmetic microscopic dynamical system should generate the continuum quantum world through a sequence of microscopic dynamics, coarse-graining, projection, amplitude emergence, and RG flow.

Seven rounds of adversarial work showed that this formulation was valuable as a stress test but too narrow as a statement of what the final theory must look like.

The arithmetic candidates repeatedly supplied useful discrete, p-adic, spectral, and phase structure while failing to generate the full continuum quantum machinery without importing additional structure. Those failures are not themselves a universal no-go theorem. They are evidence that **generation may not be the correct role to assign arithmetic**.

The v7 program therefore changes the foundational question.

It no longer asks only:

$$
oxed{
	ext{Can arithmetic generate continuum quantum physics?}
}
$$

It asks the broader and more fundamental question:

$$
oxed{
	ext{What is the smallest common mathematical structure in which
continuum physics and arithmetic structure can coexist, constrain one another,
and yield testable consequences without either being silently identified with the other?}
}
$$

The revised hypothesis has four structural commitments, none yet promoted to established physics:

1. **Adelic/local-global possibility:** real and non-Archimedean sectors are treated as mathematically distinct local sectors that may participate in one global compatibility structure.
2. **Arithmetic admissibility:** arithmetic may select or constrain physically realizable states/configurations rather than generate the continuum itself.
3. **Physical bridge:** where arithmetic has causal influence, the bridge must be represented explicitly as a constraint, interaction, reconstruction, duality, or other typed relation—not as an ambiguous "projection."
4. **Scale closure:** the resulting effective theory must possess a controlled scale dependence; Asymptotic Safety is a candidate UV organizing principle, not an axiom.

Bohmian Mechanics is not a competing microscopic substrate in this formulation. It is the candidate **IR ontological representation** of the quantum sector.

The architecture is therefore:

$$
oxed{
	ext{global/local structure}
ightarrow
	ext{admissibility and/or coupling}
ightarrow
	ext{continuum quantum sector}
ightarrow
	ext{BM representation}
ightarrow
	ext{QFT / gravity}
ightarrow
	ext{RG / AS UV structure}
}
$$

The arrows remain theorem targets, not facts.

# 1. The GR-Refines-Newton Lesson, Recast

General relativity did not become deeper merely by adding more equations to Newtonian mechanics.

Its strength came from changing the underlying conceptual structure while recovering Newton in the regime where Newton had already succeeded.

The research program now adopts the same methodological lesson:

1. **Preserve successful phenomenology.**
2. **Remove only assumptions that are not forced by observation or theorem.**
3. **Replace those assumptions with a broader structure rather than a new collection of special-case mechanisms.**
4. **Produce a new consequence that the older description did not require.**

Our problem is different from Newton/GR in one critical respect:

**we do not yet have a Mercury.**

We have candidate exact-discrete corners, candidate arithmetic selection mechanisms, and candidate Bell/information-capacity channels. None is allowed to be called an anomaly until a quantitative residual or selection rule is actually derived.

The required refinement is therefore:

$$
oxed{
	ext{successful continuum QM}
+
	ext{new arithmetic structure}
=
	ext{testable refinement}
}
$$

not:

$$
	ext{successful continuum QM}
=
	ext{evidence that arithmetic must already be there}.
$$

# 5. The Adelic Principle

## 5.1 Mathematical starting point

The rational field has an Archimedean completion and non-Archimedean completions:

$$
\mathbb Q\to\mathbb R,
\qquad
\mathbb Q\to\mathbb Q_p.
$$

The adele ring is

$$
\mathbb A_{\mathbb Q}
=
\mathbb R
\times
\prod_p' \mathbb Q_p.
$$

The restricted product is the standard number-theoretic structure. The individual $\mathbb Q_p$ are not discrete topological spaces; finite quotients such as

$$
\mathbb Z_p/p^L\mathbb Z_p
$$

are finite discrete approximants.

v7 therefore uses:

$$
\boxed{
\text{Archimedean}
\leftrightarrow
\text{non-Archimedean}
}
$$

as a local/completion distinction, not as "continuous versus discrete" in a literal topological sense.

## 5.2 Physical hypothesis

The program permits, but does not assume, that physical data admit local descriptions

$$
\mathfrak U_v,
\qquad
v=\infty,2,3,5,\ldots
$$

with a globally admissible subset

$$
\mathfrak U_{\rm global}
\subseteq
\prod_v' \mathfrak U_v.
$$

The global condition is the physical candidate.

The existence of local sectors by itself is not the physical claim.

## 5.3 No automatic Hasse principle

A Hasse-principle analogy is allowed only where the actual mathematical problem has the required local-global theorem.

The program therefore uses the neutral phrase:

$$
\boxed{\text{adelic local-global compatibility}}
$$

and introduces Hasse principles, strong approximation, reciprocity, or adelic harmonic analysis only when their hypotheses actually apply.

## 5.4 Global character structure

Adelic harmonic analysis supplies a mathematically precise precedent for global/local character factorization.

Schematically:

$$
\chi_{\mathbb A}(x)
=
\chi_\infty(x_\infty)
\prod_p\chi_p(x_p).
$$

This is useful because the candidate arithmetic phase sector can be formulated through characters rather than through an analogy between a p-adic label and a complex phase.

But:

$$
\boxed{
\text{mathematical character}
\neq
\text{physical quantum phase}
}
$$

unless a physical bridge is demonstrated.

# 7. The Bridge Is Not One Thing

The word "bridge" was too broad in v6.3.1.

v7 defines five bridge classes.

## B1 — Compatibility

A global relation ties local sectors together.

$$
C_{\rm global}(\omega_\infty,\omega_2,\ldots)=0.
$$

No dynamical interaction is implied.

## B2 — Constraint / admissibility

Arithmetic restricts physical states:

$$
P_{\rm ar}\psi=\psi.
$$

No force or energy exchange is implied.

## B3 — Physical interaction

Arithmetic observables couple directly to continuum observables:

$$
H
=
H_\infty\otimes I
+
I\otimes H_{\rm ar}
+
H_{\rm int},
$$

with candidate

$$
H_{\rm int}
=
\sum_a g_a A_a^{\rm ar}\otimes B_a^{\rm cont}.
$$

This is only one candidate realization.

## B4 — Reconstruction / conditional access

The continuum description may be reconstructed from arithmetic data, or arithmetic information may be encoded in a different representation.

A candidate may use:

- conditional expectation;
- operator-algebraic inclusion;
- holographic-style reconstruction;
- error-correcting reconstruction;
- state reconstruction.

## B5 — Duality / correspondence

Two mathematically distinct descriptions may represent the same physical theory:

$$
\mathfrak U_1
\simeq_{\rm phys}
\mathfrak U_2.
$$

Duality is not interaction.

An adelic relation is not automatically duality.

A projection is not automatically reconstruction.

A tensor product is not automatically coupling.

These distinctions are foundational in v7.

Writing

$$

$$
\boxed{
\exists\,O_\infty

$$
\omega_{\rm ar}^{(1)}\neq\omega_{\rm ar}^{(2)}

\langle O_\infty\rangle_1
\neq

An even stronger quantum test is entanglement generation from an initially separable state.

Therefore v7 distinguishes:

\text{conditional dynamical influence}

\text{genuine quantum interaction}

as different structural claims.

That was correct for the old hypothesis.

The revised hypothesis changes the question.

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{
\text{complex amplitude existence}
\neq
\text{arithmetic ownership of amplitude}.

The arithmetic sector can be physically meaningful even if it does not generate $R$.

This does not make the amplitude problem irrelevant.

It changes what must be proven.

The remaining questions are:

1. Can the arithmetic structure remain consistent with complex amplitudes?

# Appendix B — v7 Minimum Promotion Standard

A proposed physical claim must now provide:

1. a typed mathematical object;
2. the exact hypotheses under which it exists;
3. its representation class;
4. the strongest appropriate null;
5. an ablation;
6. an ownership classification;
7. an observable or universal consequence;
8. a preregistered discriminator;
9. an independent review;
10. an explicit kill condition.

The promotion chain is:

$$
\boxed{
\text{idea}
\rightarrow
\text{formal object}
\rightarrow
\text{null}
\rightarrow
\text{ablation}
\rightarrow
\text{ownership}
\rightarrow
\text{prediction}
\rightarrow
\text{reality}
}
$$

Promotion never means truth.

It means only:

> **the claim has earned the right to be tested as physical structure rather than treated as speculation.**
